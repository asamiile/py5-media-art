# dictyostelium_camp_spiral_aggregation

An animated 3D visualization of the multicellular aggregation of *Dictyostelium discoideum* slime mold cells. Chemotactic movement is guided by rotating waves of cyclic AMP (cAMP).

## Details

- **Date**: 2026-08-11
- **Theme**: Chemotactic multicellular aggregation of Dictyostelium discoideum cells. Rotating spiral waves of cyclic AMP guide individual cells as they coordinate and stream into branching patterns.
- **Technique**: 3D particle system projected mathematically onto a 2D canvas with depth sorting (Painter's Algorithm) and depth fading. Cell particles follow a combined force field of radial attraction, spiral wavefront flow, and local cell-cell cohesion, forming organic networks of branching streams.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Background: Deep slate-charcoal (`#111215`)
  - Dominant: Bioluminescent amber/yellow cells (`#ffaa1d`, `#ffd700`)
  - Accent: Deep magenta cAMP waves (`#ff1493`) and hot-spot white cores (`#ffffff`)
- **Format**: Animation (60fps)
