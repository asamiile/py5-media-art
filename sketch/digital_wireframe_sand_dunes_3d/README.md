## Metadata
- **Date**: 2026-06-07
- **Theme**: A minimalist landscape of rolling sand dunes made entirely of glowing digital wireframes that shift 
- **Technique**: A triangle strip mesh is rendered over a grid. The Z-height of each vertex is determined by a moving 3D OpenSimplex noise field, giving the illusion of flying over shifting dunes. The wireframes fade into the distance via alpha mapping, and colors gently shift over time.

## Concept
A minimalist landscape of rolling sand dunes made entirely of glowing digital wireframes that shift endlessly like a desert swept by a data storm.
