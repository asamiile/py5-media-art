# moire_lattice_resonance

- **Theme**: Twisted bilayer graphene, Moiré superlattices, magic angles, topological flat bands.
- **Technique**: 3D-like particle simulation of electron tracers in a Moiré potential field. The potential is derived from the interference of reciprocal lattice vectors of two hexagonal grids with a dynamic twist angle $\theta$.
- **Aesthetic**: "Electric Emerald" and "Ultraviolet" spectral mapping with a high-density "silken" particle flow and a subtle background starfield.
- **Format**: 3840x2160 (4K) | 60fps | 20 seconds.

## Background

When two layers of hexagonal lattices (like graphene) are stacked with a small relative rotation, a large-scale interference pattern called a **Moiré pattern** emerges. At certain "magic angles," the electronic structure changes drastically, leading to flat bands where electrons become highly localized and exhibit strong correlations (superconductivity, magnetism). This work visualizes the dynamic transition between these states as the twist angle oscillates.
