# liquid_topology

A mesmerizing, shimmering field of liquid silver that ripples and flows across the entire canvas. Light catches the viscous peaks in electric cyan and deep violet, creating a sense of immense depth and complexity as the topological surface deforms.

## Concept
Fluid intelligence, liquid data, topological deformation, metallic reflection. The work explores the metaphor of a living, shifting information ocean.

## Technique
- **Noise Terrain**: Multi-octave 3D noise field generated using vectorized NumPy for smooth undulations.
- **Domain Warping**: The noise field is iteratively distorted to create the characteristic viscous flow of heavy liquids.
- **Metallic Shading**: High-density vertex mesh in P3D with multi-source specular and directional lighting to simulate chrome/mercury.
- **Viscosity**: Slow-motion advection of the noise field coordinates to simulate fluid inertia.
