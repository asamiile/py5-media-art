# quantum_hall_edge_states

A precise and technically stunning visualization of topological protection in a 2D electron gas. Within the deep indigo bulk, particles are trapped in localized cyclotron loops, while along the boundaries, they form a swift, unidirectional current of shimmering electric lime light.

## Concept
- **Theme**: Quantum Hall Effect, topological protection, chiral edge states, topological insulators.
- **Visuals**: The striking contrast between the "insulating" interior (localized cyclotron loops) and the "conducting" boundaries (unidirectional skipping orbits). This represents the core principle of topological phases of matter.

## Technical Details
- **Technique**: 2D particle simulation of 150,000 charge carriers under a strong perpendicular magnetic field $B$.
- **Physics**: Particles follow the Lorentz force $\vec{F} = q(\vec{v} \times \vec{B})$ in the bulk. Near the boundaries, a confinement potential $\vec{E} = -\nabla U$ prevents escape, forcing the orbits to "skip" and form a chiral edge current.
- **Rendering**: Multi-pass additive rendering with a "Cobalt / Electric Lime" palette. The topologically protected edge states are rendered with enhanced glow and sharpness.
- **Output**: 15s @ 60fps 4K MP4.
