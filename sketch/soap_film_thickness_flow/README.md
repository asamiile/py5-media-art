# soap_film_thickness_flow

10-second 4K/60fps py5 animation of draining soap film where thickness variations become shifting interference colors, pale bubble rims, and dark rupture memories.

The renderer synthesizes a procedural thin-film thickness field from drainage, shear waves, and circular bubble boundaries. Spectral cosine bands, black-film dry memory, and bright rim highlights are written through py5's pixel buffer, encoded as `output.mp4`, mirrored to `soap_film_thickness_flow.mp4`, and sampled at mid-frame for `soap_film_thickness_flow_p1.png`.
