# recursive_membranes

A 3D generative animation of dimensional folding, exploring the concept of iridescent surfaces and organic geometry.

## Concept
The work visualizes a shimmering, translucent veil of light that folds and pulses in a deep cosmic void. Using a 3D noise-warped mesh algorithm, the piece creates complex, organic membranes that shimmer with iridescent light. The height-based spectral mapping and translucent layering create an intricate, ethereal tapestry of geometric resonance under a silent, star-dusted night sky.

## Technique
- **3D Noise-Warped Mesh (P3D)**: A large, translucent mesh (80x80 grid) distorted by multi-octave Simplex noise and sinusoidal folding, creating organic, dimensional forms.
- **Iridescent Curvature Mapping**: Height-based HSB mapping shifts the membrane's color from Electric Cyan to Royal Amethyst based on local displacement.
- **Translucent Layering**: Rendering multiple overlapping instances of the membrane with slight phase offsets and low alpha creates a shimmering, pearlescent texture.
- **High-Density Starfield**: A shimmering starfield background reinforces the cosmic/astronomical scale, addressing the "beautiful night sky" request.

## Palette
- **Membrane**: Iridescent Cyan, Royal Amethyst, Pale Lavender
- **Highlights**: Rose-Gold Sparkle
- **Void**: Midnight Blue/Black
