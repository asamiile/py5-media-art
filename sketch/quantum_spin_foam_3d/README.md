# quantum_spin_foam_3d

## Concept
A microscopic look at quantum spin foam, represented as interlocking 3D rings that constantly flip orientations and flash brightly when they connect.

## Technique
3D generative layout of `py5.ellipse` objects using `py5.rotate_x` and `py5.rotate_y` driven by Perlin noise, creating a shifting interconnected mesh of quantum loops.
