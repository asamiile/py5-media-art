# quantum_interference_glitch_3d

## Details
- **Date**: 2026-06-03
- **Format**: Animation (15s @ 60fps)
- **Theme**: An abstract visualization of quantum interference patterns. Two large overlapping wave systems intersecting, causing destructive and constructive interference, depicted through thousands of tiny glowing cubes that change color and height based on the wave amplitude.
- **Technique**: A 2D grid rendered in 3D where each cell's Z-height is determined by the sum of two expanding sine waves (interference). The amplitude also dictates the color and the `py5.box()` size, giving a tactile, volumetric feel to a mathematical concept. Random jitter and scale spikes add a glitch aesthetic.
- **Color palette**:
  - Background: Stark charcoal
  - Dominant (60%): Quantum Cyan
  - Secondary (30%): Deep Indigo
  - Accent (10%): Hot Pink (constructive peaks)
  - Mood: analytical / glitchy

## Description
An animated 3D simulation of two intersecting sine waves creating a complex interference pattern on a massive grid of glowing cubes. As the waves ripple outward, constructive and destructive interference alters the color and height of the cubes. Random glitches occasionally strike individual nodes, making them flash white and spike vertically.
