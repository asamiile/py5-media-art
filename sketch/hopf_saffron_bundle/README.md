# hopf_saffron_bundle

## Overview
- **Genre**: Mathematical / Abstract Topology
- **Composition Archetype**: Asymmetric Off-Center Tension
- **Motion Archetype**: Spatial Camera / Depth Traversal
- **Tonal / Palette Family**: Rich Saturated Color Field (`#0c2444`, `#123e4a`, `#78341c`, `#f4b22a`, `#eb5a38`, `#94ece6`)
- **Format**: Animation (16s @ 60fps, 3840×2160)

## Concept & Technique
A 3D stereographic projection of 90 linked circle fibers on nested Clifford tori in $S^3$, anchored near the upper-right golden-ratio focal zone and sweeping across a rich Prussian-cobalt, viridian-teal, and burnt-sienna color field. The camera performs a smooth 3D orbital and depth traversal while simultaneous 4D Clifford rotations turn the torus shells inside-out through space.
