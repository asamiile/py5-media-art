# kinetic_recursive_mandala_bloom_2d

**Date**: 2026-07-21
**Type**: Animation (900 frames, 60fps)

## Concept
An intricate, slowly blooming recursive mandala of glowing geometric shapes unfolding from the center.

## Techniques
Recursive drawing of overlapping semi-transparent polygons that rotate and expand outward. The structure modulates its geometry and colors based on time using Perlin noise, creating a highly detailed, crystalline, organic network of additive lines.

## Palette
Deep teals and cyans blending into bright neon greens, purples and whites, drawn with additive blending on a black background for a high-contrast glow.
