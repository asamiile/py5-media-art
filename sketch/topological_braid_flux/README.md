# Topological Braid Flux

A 3D simulation of topological braids weaving through a chaotic flow field, representing the rhythmic connectivity of the vacuum.

## Concept

This artwork explores the visual language of knot theory and topology. Multiple "braid threads" are evolved through a 3D volume, where their crossing patterns are determined by the interaction of a global helical flow and local chaotic perturbations (Perlin noise). The motion represents the unfolding and re-tangling of hidden dimensions.

## Technical Details

- **Braid Simulation**: 16 independent threads, each composed of 250 vertices.
- **Dynamics**: Combined helical vortex flow and noise-driven displacement. The "weaving" is achieved by phase-shifted oscillators affecting the radial and angular components of each thread.
- **Rendering**: Multi-pass additive line rendering. Each thread is drawn multiple times with varying stroke weights and transparencies to create a "glow" or "shimmer" effect.
- **Palette**: A rhythmic shift between "Deep Indigo", "Electric Cyan", and "Crimson", mapped to the spatial and temporal phase of each thread.
- **Environment**: 4K/60fps animation with a high-density background starfield (15,000 stars).

## Aesthetics

The visual impression is one of rhythmic elegance and complexity. The glowing threads weave and cross in complex patterns, creating shimmering interference where they overlap. The motion is fluid and hypnotic, suggesting a living, topological structure pulsating against the star-dusted obsidian night.
