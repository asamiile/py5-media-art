# stellar_magnetic_reconnection_3d

## Details
- **Date**: 2026-06-03
- **Format**: Animation (15s @ 60fps)
- **Theme**: The violent but beautiful snapping and reconnection of magnetic field lines in the corona of a star.
- **Technique**: 3D bezier curves connected between shifting points on a spherical surface. As points drift apart due to noise, the bezier curves stretch and abruptly snap to new closer neighbors.
- **Color palette**:
  - Background: Pitch black
  - Dominant (60%): Solar flare orange
  - Secondary (30%): Deep crimson
  - Accent (10%): Blinding white/yellow
  - Mood: dark/moody

## Description
An animated 3D simulation of magnetic reconnection where glowing orange and crimson field lines stretch and violently snap to new configurations, glowing blinding white at the reconnection points.
