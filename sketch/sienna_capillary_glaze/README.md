# sienna_capillary_glaze

## Overview
A 4K (3840×2160) generative video artwork exploring **wet-on-wet pigment chromatography, coffee-ring capillary halos, and Rothko-scale glaze fields**. Anchored asymmetrically in the upper-left golden-ratio zone, a deep lapis-ultramarine (`#143474`) and verdigris-turquoise (`#189c90`) bloom expands across a warm burnt-sienna (`#701e12`), alizarin-madder (`#a8281e`), and raw-amber (`#d66820`) color field over a 19-second (1140-frame @ 60fps) multi-act cycle.

## Visual & Technical Highlights
- **Genre**: Atmospheric / Painterly / Color Field
- **Composition Archetype**: Asymmetric Off-Center Tension
- **Motion Archetype**: Multi-Act Temporal Evolution
- **Palette**: Deep Burnt Sienna (`#701e12`), Alizarin Madder (`#a8281e`), Raw Amber Glaze (`#d66820`), Lapis Ultramarine (`#143474`), Verdigris Turquoise (`#189c90`), Naples Gold (`#f6c438`), Warm Cream (`#f9eeda`).
- **Techniques**: Domain-warped wet-on-wet pigment diffusion and capillary coffee-ring contours in GLSL (`sienna_capillary_glaze.glsl`), 210 vectorized capillary pigment veins and 420 granulation floccules batched via `py5.QUADS` / `py5.vertices`, and `PostFX` finishing.
