## Concept
Five distinct coral morphologies (branching, plate, staghorn, brain, fan) grow organically across the sea floor; vibrant coral pink, seafoam teal, golden amber, soft lavender, and bright turquoise colonies rise from deep navy waters with atmospheric light rays and rising bubbles

- **Visuals**: randomized parameters, depth-based layering, underwater lighting effects
