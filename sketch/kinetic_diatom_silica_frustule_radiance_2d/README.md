# kinetic_diatom_silica_frustule_radiance_2d

Kinetic diatom silica frustule biomineralization and polarized optical interference simulation executed in py5.

## Concept

An artistic exploration of marine phytoplankton morphology and silica biomineralization, inspired by Ernst Haeckel's *Kunstformen der Natur* (Plate 4: Diatomea) and polarized light confocal laser microscopy.

Planktonic diatoms (*Bacillariophyta*) fabricate intricate nanostructured shells (frustules) of amorphous hydrated silica ($SiO_2 \cdot nH_2O$). The artwork simulates a living centric diatom valve disk featuring 24 radial structural costae ribs, six concentric tiers of chambered loculi sectors, and thousands of perforated cribrum sieve pores. Under rotating polarized optical illumination, thin-film interference illuminates the silica facets with vibrant structural colors—electric cyan, opalescent amethyst, silica amber, and chlorophyll emerald—pulsing in 12.0s harmonic resonance across a deep hadal marine abyss.
