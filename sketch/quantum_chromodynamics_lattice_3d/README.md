# quantum_chromodynamics_lattice_3d

**Date**: 2026-06-17
**Format**: Animation (15s @ 60fps)
**Theme**: A visualization of boiling quantum foam where the strong force binds colorful quarks together in a 3D lattice, constantly snapping and reconnecting in a chaotic dance.
**Technique**: A 3D grid of particles whose positions are perturbed by a noisy 4D temperature field. Connections (simulating strong force springs) constantly break and reform based on proximity thresholds. Additive blending with RGB primaries (representing color charge) visually simulates the intense energy of quantum chromodynamics.
