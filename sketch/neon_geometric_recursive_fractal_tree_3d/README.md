# neon_geometric_recursive_fractal_tree_3d

A 3D animation of a complex geometric L-system tree growing and branching out symmetrically, with leaves/nodes pulsating with light.

## Theme
A 3D recursive branching geometric structure illuminated by shifting neon light.

## Technique
Uses recursion (`push_matrix()`, `translate()`, `rotate()`, `pop_matrix()`) to generate a 3D branching structure up to a depth of 8. The branching angles are driven by slow sine waves, making the neon fractal tree continuously sway and bloom over time while the camera revolves around it.
