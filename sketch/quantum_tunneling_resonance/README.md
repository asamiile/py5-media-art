# quantum_tunneling_resonance

A 3D generative media art piece visualizing the phenomenon of quantum tunneling through a potential barrier.

## Concept
The work represents a quantum wave packet—a shimmering cloud of iridescent particles—colliding with an invisible potential barrier. While the majority of the energy is reflected in complex interference patterns of electric violet and cyan, a fraction of the wavefunction "tunnels" through the forbidden region, emerging as a needle-sharp, high-energy beam of white-gold light that accelerates into the obsidian void.

## Technical Details
- **Simulation**: 160,000 particles modeling wave-packet probability density. Implements reflection (interference) and tunneling ($T \approx 0.05$) logic at a potential barrier ($z=0$).
- **Rendering**: Multi-pass additive rendering with HSB spectral mapping (Violet/Cyan/Gold).
- **Starfield**: High-density background of 12,000 stars.
- **Resolution**: 3840x2160 (4K) @ 60fps.
- **Duration**: 10 seconds.

## Aesthetic
- **Theme**: Quantum tunneling, wave packet interference, potential barrier, beautiful night sky.
- **Visuals**: Iridescent rippling waves, interference fringes, needle-sharp white-gold tunneling beams against a deep star-dusted night.
