# supersolid_lattice_vibration

- **Date**: 2026-05-12
- **Theme**: Quantum matter, supersolids, spontaneous translational symmetry breaking, phonon-roton modes.
- **Visual Impression**: A crystal-like 3D lattice of crystalline droplets—each a miniature Bose-Einstein condensate—shimmers in a cold, dark vacuum. As a "roton" excitation passes through, the droplets begin to pulse and vibrate in a coordinated, wave-like dance. The colors shift between "Arctic Silver" and "Aurora Green" as the density fluctuates, creating a sense of a material that is both a solid and a superfluid.
- **Technique**: 3D lattice simulation. Each droplet is composed of a dense cluster of particles (total 100,000 particles). Lattice vibrations modeled as coupled oscillators or wave propagation. Multi-pass additive rendering with HSB spectral shifts. Palette: "Crystalline Blue / Arctic Silver / Aurora Green". 15s 4K/60fps MP4 (1080p source).
