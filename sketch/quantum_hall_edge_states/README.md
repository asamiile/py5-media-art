- **Technique**: 2D particle simulation of 150,000 charge carriers under a strong perpendicular magnetic field. Features multi-pass additive rendering with "Cobalt / Electric Lime" palette. 60fps high-bitrate MP4.
- **Logic Lab Reference**: None

## Concept
A precise and technically stunning visualization of topological protection in a 2D electron gas. Within the deep indigo bulk, particles are trapped in localized cyclotron loops, while along the boundaries, they form a swift, unidirectional current of shimmering electric lime light.

## Technical Details
- **Renderer**: P3D
- **Simulation**: particle.
- **Visuals**: additive blending, HSB spectral mapping, bloom-like highlights, prismatic color, dark-field contrast.
- **Animation**: 15 seconds at 60fps
