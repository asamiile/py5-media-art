# kinetic_fractal_noise_flowfield_2d

**Date**: 2026-07-21
**Type**: Animation (900 frames, 60fps)

## Concept
Thousands of tiny particles swept away by an unseen chaotic magnetic field.

## Techniques
Curl noise flow field driving 25,000 small particles. The motion of the particles is governed by a curl field derived from Perlin noise derivatives, generating overlapping long trails via a slow-fading alpha background.

## Palette
High contrast electric pink and purple trails mapped by angle, drawn onto a pitch-black background.
