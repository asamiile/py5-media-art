# primordial_polarization_swirl

A majestic visualization of the early universe's first light; silken swirls of celestial azure and soft gold energy are twisted into intricate B-mode patterns by primordial gravitational waves, shimmering across a deep amethyst void.

## Concept
- **Theme**: B-mode polarization, primordial gravitational waves, Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB), inflationary cosmology.
- **Visuals**: A shimmering "shell" of light that appears to be twisted into intricate, silken swirls by invisible tensor perturbations. The motion is slow and high-dimensional.

## Technical Details
- **Technique**: 3D simulation of 120,000 particles on a spherical shell advected by a multi-scale "curl" vector field representing tensor perturbations.
- **Field**: $\vec{v} = \nabla \Phi \times \vec{r}$ where $\Phi$ is a multi-harmonic scalar potential. This guarantees the flow is tangent to the sphere.
- **Expansion**: Slow radial scaling simulates the expanding universe.
- **Rendering**: Multi-pass additive point rendering with HSB spectral mapping.
- **Output**: 12s @ 60fps 4K MP4.
