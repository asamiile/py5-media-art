# kinetic_weibel_instability_collisionless_shock_2d

2D generative media art depicting the spontaneous generation of intense microscopic magnetic filaments in relativistic counter-streaming collisionless plasmas via the Weibel electromagnetic instability, culminating in a turbulent collisionless shock front with diffusive Fermi particle acceleration.

## Physics & Generative Concept

In extreme astrophysical environments (such as gamma-ray burst jets, pulsar wind nebulae, and relativistic supernova blast waves), plasma clouds collide at velocities approaching the speed of light ($v \sim c$). Because the Coulomb mean free path is astronomical in scale, these plasmas cannot form ordinary collisional hydrodynamic shocks.

Instead, the shock is mediated entirely by collective electromagnetic micro-instabilities:

1. **Weibel Instability & Current Filamentation**: Transverse electromagnetic perturbations spontaneously deflect counter-streaming electron and ion beams, self-organizing them into alternating longitudinal current filaments ($J_x$) separated by the electron skin depth ($d_e = c/\omega_{pe}$).
2. **Magnetic Bennett Pinching**: The magnetic field $B_z$ generated by these current tubes exerts a self-pinching Lorentz force ($\mathbf{J} \times \mathbf{B}$), concentrating the current into dense relativistic threads.
3. **Kink Instability & Filament Coalescence**: Parallel current filaments attract and merge via magnetic reconnection and kink undulations, transferring magnetic energy from microscopic scales to macroscopic structures.
4. **Collisionless Shock Formation & Fermi Acceleration**: Near the collision boundary ($x \approx 0$), the accumulated magnetic barrier halts and thermalizes the incoming beams into an isotropic downstream magnetic turbulence core. Relativistic particles undergo pitch-angle scattering and diffusive shock acceleration (Fermi acceleration), producing power-law cosmic ray sparks.

## Technical Implementation

- **Relativistic Field Formulation**: 2D continuous Weibel magnetic field $B_z(x, y)$ computed on a $480 \times 270$ numerical grid with skin-depth harmonic modes, shock compression envelope, and post-shock turbulence.
- **Current Density Derivation**: Vectorized finite-difference curl $J_x = \frac{c}{4\pi} \frac{\partial B_z}{\partial y}$ identifying pinched current channels.
- **Dual-Light Specular Shading**: Surface normal mapping illuminated by two directional light vectors, creating an iridescent metallic plasma sheen along the filament ridges and shock crests.
- **Particle System**: 4,000 relativistic charged particles tracked through counter-streaming flow, filamentary magnetic trapping, and stochastic Fermi acceleration scattering.
- **Format**: 900 frames @ 60 FPS (15 seconds seamless loop) rendered at 1920×1080 via py5 P2D with ffmpeg H.264 master encoding.
