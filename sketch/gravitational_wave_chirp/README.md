# gravitational_wave_chirp

- **Date**: 2026-05-12
- **Theme**: General relativity, binary black hole merger, gravitational waves, space-time ripples, chirp signal.
- **Visual Impression**: Two blindingly bright points of light—binary black holes—spiral around each other in a dark, distorted space-time. As they lose energy through gravitational radiation, their orbit decays, spinning faster and faster. At the moment of coalescence, a titanic "chirp" of space-time ripples erupts, sending concentric waves of "Electric Gold" and "Deep Violet" light across the cosmos. The animation captures the final seconds of this cosmic cataclysm.
- **Technique**: 3D particle simulation. Two orbiting mass points. Space-time distortion modeled as a metric perturbation $\hbar_{\mu\nu}$ that shifts particle positions. Concentric wave emission during and after merger. Multi-pass additive rendering. Palette: "Deep Violet / Electric Gold / Star-White". 15s 4K/60fps MP4 (1080p source).
