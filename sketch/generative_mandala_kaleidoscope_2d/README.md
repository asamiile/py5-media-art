# generative_mandala_kaleidoscope_2d

**Date**: 2026-07-06
**Type**: Animation (10-30s @ 60fps)

## Concept
A generative mandala that builds itself layer by layer from the center outwards. It uses kaleidoscopic symmetry where complex procedural ornaments (petals, rings, dots) continuously morph, expand, and rotate at different speeds.

## Techniques
Radial symmetry achieved through push_matrix, rotate, and scale. The ornaments are driven by multiple overlapping sine waves (Lissajous-like parameters) to give a breathing, organic feel to the geometry.

## Palette
Neon on black. Intensely bright magenta, cyan, and yellow layers that overlap with additive blending (screen/add mode).
