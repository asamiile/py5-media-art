# abstract_optical_illusion_moire_2d

## Concept
A geometric animation that explores optical illusions through the creation of dynamic Moiré patterns. By overlapping and independently rotating/translating dense arrays of concentric rings and radial lines, it produces hypnotic, emergent interference patterns that trick the eye and create a sense of continuous motion and depth.

## Technique
The sketch generates multiple layers of dense geometric primitives: concentric rings and radial lines spanning outwards from the center. By subtly animating the rotation angles and the center offsets of these different layers over time using sine and cosine functions, the overlapping lines intersect at varying angles, naturally producing complex Moiré interference patterns.

## Palette
- **Background**: Cream (warm, neutral base)
- **Primary Patterns**: Deep Black (high contrast interference)
- **Accents**: Crimson Red & Royal Blue (adding depth and color shifting to the optical illusion)
- **Mood**: Hypnotic, geometric, dynamic, abstract

## Format
Animation (900 frames @ 60fps)
