# basalt_fault_strata

## Overview
- **Genre**: Architectural / Brutalism / Geometric
- **Composition Archetype**: Stratified Horizon / Diagonal Sweep
- **Motion Archetype**: Directional Advection & Traveling Wavefronts
- **Tonal / Palette Family**: Stark Constructivist High-Contrast (`#0f1116`, `#f3f0e8`, `#e83424`, `#1c3ed6`, `#f2b218`)
- **Format**: Animation (22s @ 60fps, 3840×2160)

## Concept & Technique
A diagonally stratified tectonic fault and columnar shear landscape sweeping at a $-24^\circ$ angle across a stark constructivist carbon-black ground (`#0f1116`). Twenty-two counter-advecting basalt horizons and 308 foreground constructivist fault blocks in crisp bone white, signal vermilion, international ultramarine, and cadmium ochre slip and interlock along propagating transverse shear waves.
