# recursive_interference

An exploration of multi-scale interference patterns generated by a dynamic quadtree subdivision. Thousands of recursive emitters pulse and intersect, creating a dense, shimmering tapestry of light that feels both biological and highly algorithmic.

## Details

- **Theme**: Recursive physics, multi-scale interference, digital optics, complex resonance.
- **Technique**: Quadtree subdivision driven by moving noise, persistent PGraphics accumulation for temporal glow, triple-shard interference emission, additive spectral highlights.
- **Palette**: Deep Navy Night background; spectral shards transitioning from Electric Teal to Stellar Orange/Pink; high-density twinkle starfield.
