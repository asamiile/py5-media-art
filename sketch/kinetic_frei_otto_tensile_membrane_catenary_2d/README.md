# kinetic_frei_otto_tensile_membrane_catenary_2d

Kinetic Frei Otto tensile membrane architecture and catenary cable net simulation executed in py5.

## Concept

An artistic exploration of lightweight tensile architecture, minimal soap-film surface form-finding, and structural cable-net mechanics, inspired by the groundbreaking work of Frei Otto (Institute for Lightweight Structures / IL Stuttgart; Munich Olympic Stadium 1972).

Tensile architecture relies on doubly curved anticlastic (saddle-shaped) geometries where orthogonal cable systems—sagging suspension cables and arching stabilizing cables—maintain continuous prestress under aerodynamic wind forces. The artwork simulates a dual-bay interconnected hyperbolic paraboloid (hypar) canopy rendered on warm architectural drafting vellum. Translucent PTFE fabric facets catch dynamic sun-angle illumination, tensioned between articulated steel tubular kingposts, radiating guy wire tiebacks, and heavy catenary boundary wire ropes (*Seilnetzränder*) in 12.0s harmonic aeroelastic resonance.
