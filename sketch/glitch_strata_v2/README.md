## Concept
A majestic, high-fidelity refinement of the `glitch_strata` concept; a vertical cross-section of luxury data-memory is rendered as an elegant, shimmering tapestry of obsidian, deep gold, and pale amber. Intricate horizontal displacement mapping and block corruption patterns create a sense of structured digital archaeology, revealing the hidden beauty of corrupted information against a silent, star-dusted night.
