# ambient_lysosomal_degradation_3d

Simulating lysosomal degradation, where cellular waste is enveloped and broken down into reusable building blocks (inspired by GO:0005764 - lysosome and GO:0009056 - catabolic process).

## Details

- **Type**: 3D animation
- **Length**: 10 seconds (60fps)

## Technique

A 3D sketch featuring a large, faintly glowing spherical boundary representing a lysosome. Inside, hundreds of pink, jittery spheres (acid hydrolases/enzymes) orbit continuously. Geometric cellular debris structures pass through the lysosome; as they approach the center, they dissolve and shatter, their vertices scattering and transforming into bright, isolated yellow-green orbs representing recovered amino acids and nucleotides. Rendered with additive blending for a highly organic, energy-rich feel.
