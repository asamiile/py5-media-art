# kinetic_lattice_gas_fluid_dynamics_2d

An animated 2D fluid dynamics visualization simulating a Lattice Gas Automaton (LGA) based on HPP rules past a circular cylinder obstacle.

## Details

- **Date**: 2026-08-11
- **Theme**: A stream of discrete molecules flowing past a cylinder, scattering, and forming wake patterns in a cybernetic wind tunnel.
- **Technique**: Vectorized 2D HPP Lattice Gas Automaton. Implements streaming, bounce-back reflections, and head-on scattering collisions on a discrete 2D grid. The resulting local density field is smoothed and rendered as a glowing fluid field, highlighting the obstacle boundary.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Background: Obsidian Abyss (`#020205`)
  - Dominant: Electric Blue (`#1e90ff`)
  - Secondary: Bright Mint (`#3eb489`)
  - Accent: Solar Gold (`#ffd700`)
- **Format**: Animation (60fps)
