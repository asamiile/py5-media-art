# gasket_metropolis

A dense, shimmering metropolis of circular skyscrapers generated through fractal subdivision.

## Concept

This work explores the intersection of fractal geometry and urban planning. By recursively subdividing space into circular domains and extruding them into 3D forms, it creates a sense of infinite density and architectural complexity. The neon highlights and star-dusted background give it a "modern city feel" within a cosmic context.

## Technique

- **Recursive Circle Subdivision**: A custom algorithm that fills space with decreasingly smaller circles, creating a fractal-like distribution.
- **3D Isometric Extrusion**: Each circular domain is extruded into a 3D prism using the P3D renderer, with heights randomized to simulate a skyline.
- **Neon Spectral Lighting**: A high-contrast palette of electric blue, laser pink, and cyber lime is used for edge highlights, creating a vibrant, glowing atmosphere.
- **Perspective and Motion**: A slow rotation around the vertical axis provides a sense of scale and depth, revealing the complex interlocking geometry of the "living blocks."

## Palette

- **Obsidian**: The dark foundation of the digital city.
- **Electric Blue**: Data hubs and cooling towers.
- **Laser Pink**: Residential and commercial pulses.
- **Cyber Lime**: Energy conduits and parks.
- **Neon Amber**: Communication nodes.
