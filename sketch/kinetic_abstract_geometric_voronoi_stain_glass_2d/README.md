# kinetic_abstract_geometric_voronoi_stain_glass_2d

A continuously shifting and morphing Voronoi diagram that resembles liquid, animated stained glass.

## Techniques

Uses SciPy's `Voronoi` to compute the geometric regions of 100 points wandering through a Perlin noise field. A ring of phantom points ensures all visible regions remain finite and renderable.

## Palette

Deep, rich stained glass hues (reds, blues, yellows, purples) with bold, dark dividing lines.
