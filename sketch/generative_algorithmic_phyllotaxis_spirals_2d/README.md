# generative_algorithmic_phyllotaxis_spirals_2d

An animated 15s sequence of generative algorithmic phyllotaxis spirals in 2D.

## Concept

Intricate mathematical spirals based on the golden ratio (phyllotaxis) that bloom outwards, expanding and shifting colors, reminiscent of sunflowers or pinecones but glowing neon.

## Technique

Iterative drawing of points using polar coordinates based on the golden angle ($137.5^\circ$). Points are drawn progressively each frame, and the underlying angle is slightly animated over time to create a twisting, breathing effect as the spiral expands.

## Palette

- Background: Deep gray/black (10, 10, 10)
- Spirals: Glowing shifting neon spectrum with high saturation and brightness
