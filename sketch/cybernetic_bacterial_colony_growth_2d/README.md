## Metadata
- **Date**: 2026-06-20
- **Theme**: A high-density simulation of a cybernetic bacterial colony expanding outward, fighting for resources
- **Technique**: 2D agent-based growth where agents leave thick, brightly colored trails. Old trails slowly decay and change color from bright neon to a rusty dark red. The simulation utilizes Py5 drawing functions combined with age-based trail mapping to simulate both the expansion of life and the necrotic decay of old data structures.
