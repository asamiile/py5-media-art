# brass_celestial_orrery_3d

## Details
- **Date**: 2026-06-03
- **Format**: Animation (15s @ 60fps)
- **Theme**: A complex, intricately geared celestial orrery (a mechanical model of a solar system) made of polished brass and copper.
- **Technique**: Constructing a nested hierarchy of 3D rotations using matrix transformations. The gears and rings are drawn procedurally using `py5.TRIANGLE_STRIP` and `py5.QUAD_STRIP`. Warm, metallic directional lighting creates specular highlights and deep shadows, giving the illusion of solid brass and tarnished copper rotating against a dark, velvety background. A faint wireframe sphere surrounds the entire mechanism like a celestial cage.
- **Color palette**:
  - Background: Velvety Burgundy/Black
  - Dominant (60%): Polished Brass / Gold
  - Secondary (30%): Tarnished Copper / Bronze
  - Accent (10%): Lapis Lazuli Blue (the central planet/sun)
  - Mood: antique / mechanical / cosmic

## Description
An animated 3D simulation of an antique celestial orrery. At the center spins a sphere of deep lapis lazuli, surrounded by a complex array of interlocking brass rings, copper gears, and orbiting planetary bodies. The mechanism is illuminated by warm, directional light that catches the edges of the metallic geometry as the rings rotate at varying speeds on multiple inclined axes. A faint golden wireframe cage surrounds the entire cosmic clockwork.
