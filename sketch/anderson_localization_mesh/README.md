# anderson_localization_mesh

- **Date**: 2026-05-12
- **Theme**: Quantum physics, Anderson localization, disordered systems, metal-insulator transition.
- **Visual Impression**: A vast, distorted 3D grid of silver threads floats in the dark. At its heart, a blindingly bright, spherical concentration of electric blue and amethyst particles pulses with high frequency—the localized wavefunction. Towards the edges, the particles thin out and vanish into the disordered lattice, illustrating how disorder prevents the spread of quantum states.
- **Technique**: 3D grid simulation. 150,000 particles. Grid nodes are randomly perturbed. A "localization" function (exponential falloff) determines particle density. Manual 3D-to-2D projection. Palette: "Silver / Electric Blue / Ghostly Amethyst / Deep Cobalt". 15s 4K/60fps MP4 (1080p source).
