# topological_quantum_braid

A majestic, shimmering vision of a quantum fluid; silken filaments of electric emerald and cyan energy swirl and knot into a complex trefoil braid that pulses with hidden resonance against the star-dusted obsidian void.

## Concept

This work explores the **topology of quantum fluids**, such as superfluids or Bose-Einstein condensates, where vortices can form stable, knotted structures. Specifically, it visualizes the velocity field generated by a trefoil knot vortex filament—a fundamental example of a knotted singularity in a fluid.

## Technique

- **Knotted Vortex Simulation**: A 3D Trefoil knot serves as the core singular filament of the fluid.
- **Biot-Savart Advection**: 150,000 particles are advected using the Biot-Savart law, which describes the velocity field induced by a vortex filament.
- **Vectorized NumPy Optimization**: Uses broadcasting and chunked point sampling to calculate the complex velocity field efficiently.
- **Multi-Pass Additive Rendering**: Captures the silken, luminous quality of the fluid with an HSB palette of emerald, cyan, and indigo.
- **4K Animation**: 15 seconds at 60fps, rendered with FFmpeg.

## Visuals

- **Palette**: Neon Deep Sea (Emerald/Cyan/Indigo) that emphasizes the organic yet crystalline structure of the knotted fluid.
- **Motion**: The particles swirl around the knotted core, while the knot itself slowly rotates and braids, creating a dynamic, ever-shifting tapestry of energy.
- **Atmosphere**: Ethereal and profound, capturing the hidden mathematical order of the microscopic world against a "beautiful night sky."
