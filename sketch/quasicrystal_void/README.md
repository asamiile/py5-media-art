# Quasicrystal Void

A mathematical exploration of aperiodic resonance and interference patterns in the cosmic vacuum.

![Preview](preview_p1.png)

## Concept
Quasicrystals are structures that are ordered but not periodic. In this work, we simulate the interference of plane waves rotated by 2π/7 to create a high-dimensional rhythmic field. This field represents the complex, non-repeating fluctuations of quantum fields or aperiodic energy distributions in deep space.

## Technical Details
- **Plane Wave Summation**: The field is computed by summing 7 sets of plane waves with relative rotational offsets.
- **NumPy Vectorization**: The entire interference field is computed as a 2D NumPy array for high-performance rendering at 1080p.
- **Iridescent Mapping**:
  - The lower range of the field is mapped to a deep obsidian void.
  - The middle range transitions through royal amethyst and electric cyan.
  - The peaks flare with stellar gold and white-gold highlights.
- **Focal Glows**: Local maxima in the interference pattern are identified and accented with additive luminous points.

## Aesthetics
- **Palette**: Deep Obsidian, Royal Amethyst, Electric Cyan, Stellar Gold.
- **Mood**: Precise, mathematical, and ethereal.
- **Symmetry**: 7-fold rotational symmetry (aperiodic).
