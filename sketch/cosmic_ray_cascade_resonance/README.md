# cosmic_ray_cascade_resonance

A majestic visualization of high-energy particle cascades in the Earth's atmosphere.

## Theme
High-energy astrophysics, cosmic ray air showers, particle physics, beautiful night sky.

## Technique
3D particle cascade simulation based on the Heitler model. A primary high-energy particle triggers a stochastic branching process, spawning secondaries (pions, muons, electrons) in a narrow relativistic cone. Features 50,000 spectral particles at the shower peak. Rendered with manual 3D-to-2D projection, multi-pass additive blending, and a "Blinding White / Electric Cyan / Deep Amethyst" palette.

## Description
A single streak of light from the top of the frame suddenly shatters into a vast, shimmering cone of spectral particles. The cascade illuminates the obsidian void for a fleeting moment, tracing the paths of thousands of secondary particles as they wash over the detector plane like ghosts of a distant cosmic explosion.
