# singularity_braiding

A 3D generative media art piece visualizing the complex dynamics of topological defects in a quantum field.

## Concept
The work represents cosmic strings or topological defects as singularities in a complex potential field. Particles are advected along the gradient of this field, creating silken, braided filaments that twist and snap in a primordial void. The interplay of moving zeros and poles creates a dynamic, ever-shifting web of energy.

## Technical Details
- **Simulation**: 150,000 particles advected along the gradient of a complex potential field $f(z) = \prod (z - z_i) / \prod (z - p_j)$ using vectorized NumPy.
- **Rendering**: Multi-pass additive rendering with HSB spectral mapping (Cyan, Amethyst, Gold). 
- **Starfield**: High-density background of 12,000 stars.
- **Resolution**: 3840x2160 (4K) @ 60fps.
- **Duration**: 10 seconds.

## Aesthetic
- **Theme**: Quantum field topology, cosmic defects, beautiful night sky.
- **Visuals**: Shimmering iridescent threads, high-contrast additive glow, deep obsidian void.
