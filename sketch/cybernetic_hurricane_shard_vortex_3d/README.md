# cybernetic_hurricane_shard_vortex_3d

An animated 15s sequence of a massive cybernetic hurricane funnel composed of glowing geometric shards.

## Theme
A massive, slow-moving storm of geometric shards over a digital ocean, resembling a cybernetic hurricane.

## Technique
A 3D particle system organized into a massive swirling funnel using cylindrical coordinates and Perlin noise for turbulence. The "ocean" below is a shifting wireframe grid. Additive blending with deep cyan and electric blue colors.
