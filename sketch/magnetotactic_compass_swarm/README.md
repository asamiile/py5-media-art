# magnetotactic_compass_swarm

Magnetotactic bacteria drift across a dark microscope slide while their internal magnetite chains pull them into alignment with a slowly rotating field.

- **Format**: Animation, 10 seconds at 60fps
- **Technique**: Vectorized bacterial swarm simulation with rotating-field alignment, local oxygen wobble, rod-shaped density deposition, magnetite core darkening, and persistent motion trails rendered through the py5 pixel buffer.
- **Palette**: Olive-black slide medium, teal bacterial bodies, pearl field traces, and amber oxygen-signal tips.
