# metabolic_nodes

A 3D generative animation of living networks, exploring the concept of organic computation and synthetic synapse.

## Concept
The work visualizes an intricate, shimmering digital network that pulses and breathes in a deep cosmic void. Using a 3D stochastic network construction algorithm, the piece creates a dense, interconnected web of nodes and synapses. Glowing pulses of energy travel through the network, suggesting an underlying digital metabolism under a silent, star-dusted night sky.

## Technique
- **3D Stochastic Network Construction (P3D)**: Generating a dense, interconnected network of nodes in 3D space using proximity-based connection logic.
- **Luminous Pulse Propagation**: Animate glowing filaments of light that travel between nodes, representing information flow and metabolic energy.
- **Dynamic Node Resonance**: Nodes pulse in scale and HSB color based on the frequency and magnitude of pulses they receive, creating a rhythmic visual heartbeat.
- **Atmospheric Depth & Camera**: A rotating camera and directional lighting create a sense of immense urban scale and dramatic depth.
- **High-Density Starfield**: A shimmering starfield background reinforces the cosmic/astronomical context, addressing the "beautiful night sky" request.
