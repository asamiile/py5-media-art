## Concept & Technique
An emergent topological phase-space portrait where 3,200 tracer filaments continuously nucleate along the four canvas edges and propagate inward along the symplectic Hamiltonian stream function $(\dot{x} = \partial H/\partial y,\; \dot{y} = -\partial H/\partial x)$ of a quasi-periodic trigonometric lattice coupled with 9 precessing Gaussian saddle-vortex poles. Persistent translucent washi-veil trail decay (`fill(236, 231, 219, 7)`) accumulates layered calligraphic ribbons of iron-gall slate, raw mineral ochre, burnt terracotta, and celadon malachite across the full-canvas warm sepiolite ground.
