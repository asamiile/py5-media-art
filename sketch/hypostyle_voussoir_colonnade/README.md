# hypostyle_voussoir_colonnade

## Overview
- **Genre**: Architectural / Brutalism / Geometric
- **Composition Archetype**: 3D Sculptural / Volumetric Space (P3D)
- **Motion Archetype**: Spatial Camera / Depth Traversal
- **Palette Family**: Risograph Overprint Spot Inks
- **Format**: Animation (`22s (1320 frames @ 60fps)`, 3840×2160 4K)
- **Technique**: `P3D + PostFX`

## Concept & Visual Architecture
`hypostyle_voussoir_colonnade` is a true P3D volumetric architectural colonnade of 22 depth-stacked hypostyle bays, each spanning three vaulted aisles composed of 28 scalloped voussoir stone wedges suspended over a warm cream Risograph paper ground (`#efe9dc`).

As a perspective camera traverses and orbits through the three-aisle colonnade, the 1,848 three-dimensional voussoir hexahedra expand radially, twist through a harmonic torsion wave, and lock back into vaulted arches. Rendered in Risograph spot-ink colors — fluorescent coral (`#f05240`), peacock teal (`#12948a`), deep federal indigo (`#1a285c`), and overprint plum (`#583e6b`) — with misregistered coral-and-indigo intrados registration wires.
