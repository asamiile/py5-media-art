# kinetic_physics_chladni_plate_resonance_2d

- **Date**: 2026-07-04
- **Type**: Animation (1200 frames, 60fps)

## Concept
Visualizing cymatics (Chladni figures). A square metal plate vibrating at resonant frequencies, causing thousands of sand particles to collect in the nodal lines where the plate is still.

## Techniques
Simulates 100,000 particles moving along the inverted gradient of the squared Chladni resonance function. The resonance modes $(m, n)$ transition smoothly over time using ease-in-out interpolation, causing the sand to dynamically reorganize into complex new geometries.

## Palette
Pitch black background representing the metal plate, with bright golden-yellow particles representing fine sand.
