# kinetic_kaleidoscope_fractal_tunnel_2d

**Date**: 2026-07-21
**Type**: Animation (900 frames, 60fps)

## Concept
An endless, psychedelic zoom into a deeply nested, rotating fractal kaleidoscope tunnel. The animation simulates continuous forward motion through a geometrically complex, twisted corridor of light.

## Techniques
- **Infinite Perspective Math**: 120 geometric layers are rendered from back to front using exponential scaling. A continuous phase shift is applied to the depth index, creating the illusion of moving forward infinitely without sudden resets.
- **Fractal Star Generation**: Instead of simple polygons, each layer is a multi-pointed star whose inner and outer radii oscillate based on its depth and angle, creating sharp, crystalline folds that morph as they approach the camera.
- **Layer Rotation**: Each layer rotates at a different, dynamic speed, causing the tunnel to twist and weave recursively like a kaleidoscope.

## Palette
A synthwave-inspired palette mapping depth to color: electric cyans in the foreground transitioning into neon magentas and deep purples at the distant vanishing point.
