# kinetic_sand_gravity_sim

## Creative Brief

### Theme
A falling simulation of hundreds of thousands of neon sand grains accumulating and tumbling down geometric steps.

### Technique
Cellular Automata falling sand simulation with physics rules for stacking and tumbling.

### Color palette
- Background: Very dark grey (almost black)
- Dominant: Hot Pink
- Secondary: Electric Blue
- Accent: Gold
- Mood: High contrast, organic motion, vibrant neon.

### Visual impression (first 3 seconds)
Brightly colored sand pouring in from the top and settling into beautiful layered dunes over geometric obstacles.

### Format
Animation (10s @ 60fps)

### Rationale
A colorful cellular automata falling sand simulation, diverging from 3D models to focus on highly pixel-dense 2D granular physics.
