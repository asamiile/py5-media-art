# scanline_overprint_matrix

## Overview
- **Genre**: Signal / Glitch / Digital Artifacts
- **Composition Archetype**: Full-Canvas Polyptych / Rhythmic Matrix
- **Motion Archetype**: Spatial Camera / Depth Traversal
- **Tonal / Palette Family**: Risograph Overprint Spot Inks (`#efe9dc`, `#f05442`, `#14968c`, `#1c2a5e`)
- **Format**: Animation (19s @ 60fps, 3840×2160)

## Concept & Technique
An edge-to-edge hierarchical signal polyptych where analog FM scanlines, quantized halftone dot matrices, stepped bit-shift rasters, and crossed frequency gratings are separated across three parallax depth planes and overprinted onto warm cream paper (`#efe9dc`) in translucent fluorescent coral, peacock teal, and deep indigo spot inks. A smooth 2.5D spatial camera glides and zooms through the multi-layered register matrix.
