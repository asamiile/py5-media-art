# cymatic_salt_nodals

## Overview
- **Genre**: Physical / Micro-Kinetic
- **Composition Archetype**: Emergent Network / Boundary-to-Interior Growth
- **Motion Archetype**: Persistent Trail Accumulation & Decay
- **Tonal / Palette Family**: Deep Nocturnal Monochrome + Single Accent (`#0d1016`, `#ece6d8`, `#e2a84b`, `#f05438`)
- **Format**: Animation (21s @ 60fps, 3840×2160)

## Concept & Technique
An acoustic cymatic plate simulation where coupled standing waves $W(x, y, t)$ continuously morph through non-integer harmonic eigenmodes $(n_1, m_1, n_2, m_2)$. A custom GLSL shader renders the brushed nocturnal obsidian-slate resonator plate and its standing-wave interference relief, while 22,000 silica, brass, and vermilion garnet sand grains are advected in NumPy down the acoustic potential gradient $\nabla |W(x, y, t)|$ from the boundary exciters into crisp, bifurcating Chladni nodal filaments.
