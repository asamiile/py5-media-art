# enneper_mineral_petals

## Overview
- **Genre**: Mathematical / Abstract Topology
- **Composition Archetype**: 3D Sculptural / Volumetric Space (P3D)
- **Motion Archetype**: Multi-Act Temporal Evolution
- **Tonal / Palette Family**: High-Key Mineral & Washi (`#ece7dc`, `#2a3941`, `#b84a31`, `#c8862a`, `#3e6d68`)
- **Format**: Animation (22s @ 60fps, 3840×2160)

## Concept & Technique
A true `P3D` volumetric minimal-surface sculpture of three generalized Enneper-Bonnet harmonic shells ($k = 2, 3, 4$) floating across a full-canvas warm alabaster washi ground (`#ece7dc`). Across a 4-act temporal progression, the surfaces unfold from compact harmonic buds, sweep continuously through their isometric Bonnet association family ($X_\theta = \cos\theta\,X_0 + \sin\theta\,X_{\pi/2}$), undergo helical petal torsion, and settle into crystalline equilibrium with iron-gall geodesic rulings.
