# kinetic_math_polygonal_vortex_2d

**Type**: Animation (15s @ 60fps)

A hypnotic, swirling vortex of sharp geometric polygons that warp and blend into a continuous tunnel of light. Concentric polygons (triangles, squares, hexagons) are drawn, and their vertices are continuously rotated and scaled using a mathematical vortex field driven by time and Perlin noise. This creates a twisted, organic geometry out of rigid shapes, all rendered with additive blending to create a deep, glowing tunnel of light.
