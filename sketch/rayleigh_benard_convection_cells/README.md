# rayleigh_benard_convection_cells

A rhythmic, shimmering visualization of self-organizing fluid dynamics driven by thermal instability.

## Theme
Fluid dynamics, thermodynamics, thermal convection, Rayleigh-Bénard instability, self-organization.

## Technique
2D particle advection simulation. Tracers are guided by a dynamic velocity field derived from a hexagonal convection roll model. FeaturesHot plumes of rising fluid and cool curtains of sinking fluid, creating a shimmering, cell-like tapestry. Employs multi-pass additive rendering with a "Molten Copper / Solar Amber / Obsidian" palette.

## Description
A dark, viscous surface begins to "boil" with the energy of hidden heat. Shimmering cells of molten copper and golden light emerge and pulse, as fluid rises in intense plumes and sinks in delicate, dark veils. This self-organized dance reveals the elegant mathematical order hidden within the chaotic motion of a heated fluid.
