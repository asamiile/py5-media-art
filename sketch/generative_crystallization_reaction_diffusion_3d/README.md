# generative_crystallization_reaction_diffusion_3d

3D geometric crystallization mimicking a reaction-diffusion/DLA system growing outward from a center point.

## Details

- **Type**: 3D animation
- **Length**: 10 seconds (60fps)

## Technique

An optimized 3D Diffusion-Limited Aggregation (DLA) algorithm. Particles spawn on a sphere and walk towards the existing crystal structure until they stick. Rendered with additive blending and neon purples/magentas to give a crystalline sci-fi aesthetic.
