# kinetic_fluid_turbulence_ribbons_2d

A simulation of high-velocity plasma streams guided by curl noise, rendered as neon glowing ribbons. The particles trace continuous paths that stretch, tear, and glow intensely under additive blending, representing the turbulent, violent energy of plasma streams colliding in a magnetic chamber.

## Tech
- **Library**: py5, NumPy
- **Concept**: Fluid turbulence, high-speed plasma, neon ribbons.
- **Palette**: Pitch black void with intense cyan, electric magenta, and bright lime green ribbons.
