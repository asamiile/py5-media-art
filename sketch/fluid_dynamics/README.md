## Concept
A mathematical fluid simulation revealing the hidden patterns of flow and turbulence; Navier-Stokes equations create organic swirling patterns that resemble ocean currents or atmospheric flow, rendered in electric cyan, warm coral, and golden amber on a deep navy background

## Technical Details
- **Renderer**: P2D
- **Simulation**: Navier-Stokes-based fluid dynamics simulation with vorticity visualization
- **Visuals**: stable fluid solver with dye advection, diffusion, projection (divergence-free velocity field), and vorticity confinement to create organic swirling patterns
