# selection_pressure

An abstract evolutionary record: generations of small phenotypes drift through a dark habitat, with the fittest lineages brightening into amber and chalk while weaker variants dissolve into the substrate.

- **Theme**: A population slowly changing shape to survive pressure from its environment.
- **Technique**: Genetic-algorithm inspired selection, mutation, phenotype glyphs, and lineage trails.
- **Logic Lab reference**: `genetic_algorithms/evolving_bloops/evolving_bloops.py`
- **Palette**: Basalt black, peat green, moss, muted amber, violet shadow, chalk highlights.
- **Format**: Still image, `preview_p1.png`.
