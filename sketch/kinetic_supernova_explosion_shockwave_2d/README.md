# kinetic_supernova_explosion_shockwave_2d

A particle simulation of a stellar explosion, with an expanding shockwave of plasma that cools into glowing gas filaments as it expands.

## Techniques

100,000 particles moving outwards with an inverse-square deceleration and random turbulence added to their velocity over time, giving it an organic, stringy, nebular look.

## Palette

Intense white/gold at the core, expanding into bright magenta and cooling into deep cyan and purple towards the edges. Additive blending against a pitch black void.
