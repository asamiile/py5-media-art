# spectral_aurora_borealis_ribbons_3d

An ethereal 3D simulation of the Aurora Borealis, consisting of sheer, glowing vertical ribbons of light that undulate organically in the polar sky.

![Preview](spectral_aurora_borealis_ribbons_3d_p1.png)

## Technique

3D `QUAD_STRIP` curtains displaced by 4D OpenSimplex noise. Colors shift smoothly between emerald green, neon violet, and magenta using HSB mapping. Additive blending gives the ethereal glow.
