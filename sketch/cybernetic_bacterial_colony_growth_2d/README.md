# cybernetic_bacterial_colony_growth_2d

A high-density simulation of a cybernetic bacterial colony expanding outward, fighting for resources, and dying out, leaving complex branching pathways.

## Details

- **Type**: 2D animation
- **Length**: 10 seconds (60fps)

## Technique

2D agent-based growth where agents leave thick, brightly colored trails. Old trails slowly decay and change color from bright neon to a rusty dark red. The simulation utilizes Py5 drawing functions combined with age-based trail mapping to simulate both the expansion of life and the necrotic decay of old data structures.
