# kinetic_constructivist_spatial_decomposition_2d

Dynamic constructivist architectural deconstruction and spatial tension on tactile ivory vellum, exploring interlocking monolithic concrete slabs, shifting soft cast shadow volumes, rhythmic terracotta and Prussian indigo counterpoints, and architectural drafting linework.

## Concept & Architecture

Departing fundamentally from microscopic scientific particle fields, this work investigates macro-scale architectural constructivism, Bauhaus tectonic balance, and De Stijl spatial deconstruction:
1. **Kinetic Modular Decomposition**: An ensemble of 46 monolithic architectural slabs, cantilevered galleries, and soaring pylons disassembles and reconfigures continuously across a 12-second (720 frames @ 60 FPS) seamless harmonic cycle.
2. **2.5D Oblique Axonometric Projection**: Built on classical 30-degree architectural axonometry, preserving geometric proportionality while conveying monumental scale without perspective distortion.
3. **Dynamic Contact Penumbras & Cast Shadows**: Directional sunlight high from the northwest projects layered soft cast shadows onto the ground datum and lower terraces, revealing spatial depths and elevations.
4. **Constructivist Chromatic Punctuation**: The monolithic brutalist limestone concrete composition is activated by bold tectonic slabs painted in deep terracotta vermilion (`#da4432`), Prussian slate indigo (`#2e506e`), and raw ochre sienna (`#e4a23c`).
5. **Architectural Drafting Linework & Hatching**: Precise millimeter grids, registration crosses, calligraphic silhouette inks, and procedural diagonal drafting cross-hatchings invoke the tactile precision of hand-drafted architectural vellum.

## Specifications

- **Format**: Seamless looping animation (720 frames @ 60 FPS, 12 seconds)
- **Resolution**: 3840×2160 (Retina 4K master)
- **Engine**: py5 (Python Processing) + custom 2.5D isometric projection + back-to-front depth sorting + polygonal shadow projection + vector drafting graphics
