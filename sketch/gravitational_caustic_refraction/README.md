# gravitational_caustic_refraction

A majestic visualization of gravitational lensing and the formation of light caustics in the deep cosmos.

## Description

Silken rays of light, passing through the powerful gravitational fields of invisible masses, are warped and focused into shimmering, sharp folds known as caustics. Shifting ribbons of Nebula Gold and Electric Azure light dance across the obsidian night, tracing the invisible geometry of space-time as it is bent by the presence of dense clusters.

## Technique

- **Physics**: 3D ray-tracing simulation of 150,000 light rays undergoing gravitational deflection using a vectorized deflection model.
- **Dynamics**: Multiple moving gravitational centers (lenses) that modulate the ray trajectories in real-time, creating evolving caustic patterns.
- **Rendering**: Multi-pass additive point rendering with an iridescent HSB palette.
- **Palette**: Nebula Gold, Electric Azure, and Diamond White.
