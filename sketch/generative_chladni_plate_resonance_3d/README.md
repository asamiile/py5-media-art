# generative_chladni_plate_resonance_3d

3D simulation of sand particles organizing into complex Chladni resonance patterns on a vibrating metal plate.

## Details

- **Type**: 3D animation
- **Length**: 10 seconds (60fps)

## Technique

A 3D physics approximation where 10,000 particles fall and bounce towards the nodes of a dynamic 2D interference standing wave equation, mapped into 3D space with a slowly rotating camera. Rendered as tiny golden spheres.
