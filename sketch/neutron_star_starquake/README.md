# neutron_star_starquake

A majestic, somber vision of a neutron star's violent surface; the dark obsidian crust shudders and splits, revealing blinding white-gold energy through jagged cracks that erupt into shimmering indigo plasma filaments against the star-dusted deep indigo void.

## Concept

Neutron stars are among the most extreme objects in the universe. Their "crust" is a lattice of nuclei under immense pressure. When the magnetic or gravitational stress becomes too high, the crust can fracture in a "starquake," releasing massive amounts of energy and flaring the magnetosphere with high-energy plasma.

## Technique

- **3D Sphere with Dynamic Fractures**: A neutron star surface rendered as a dark obsidian sphere with noise-driven "starquake" spots.
- **Magnetic Field Advection**: 150,000 particles are advected along a global dipole magnetic field with helical twisting.
- **Multi-Pass Additive Rendering**: Captures the intense, high-energy glow of the fractures and the ethereal plasma jets.
- **Vectorized Physics**: NumPy-based velocity updates for high particle counts.
- **4K Animation**: 15 seconds at 60fps, rendered with FFmpeg.

## Visuals

- **Palette**: Electric Cobalt and Royal Purple plasma filaments, with blinding White-Gold starquake cores.
- **Motion**: The crust fractures pulse with erratic intensity, while the plasma flows outward in elegant, twisting corkscrew patterns.
- **Atmosphere**: Violent yet majestic, set against a high-density "beautiful night sky" background.
