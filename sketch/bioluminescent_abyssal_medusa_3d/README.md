# bioluminescent_abyssal_medusa_3d

## Details
- **Date**: 2026-06-03
- **Format**: Animation (15s @ 60fps)
- **Theme**: A massive, ethereal deep-sea jellyfish floating in the dark abyss, composed of glowing, trailing tentacles and a pulsating translucent bell.
- **Technique**: The bell is constructed as a 3D parametric hemisphere rendered with `py5.TRIANGLE_STRIP`, using additive blending and a sine wave to create a continuous rippling motion along its edges and a rhythmic overall pulsing expansion. Hundreds of tentacles trail below it, constructed as 3D line chains whose segments sway dynamically driven by 3D OpenSimplex noise.
- **Color palette**:
  - Background: Abyssal Black
  - Dominant (60%): Ghostly Translucent White
  - Secondary (30%): Bioluminescent Cyan (inner tentacles and top lighting)
  - Accent (10%): Deep Violet (outer tentacles and underlighting)
  - Mood: ethereal / organic / abyssal

## Description
An animated 3D simulation of a gigantic, bioluminescent jellyfish drifting through the abyssal depths. The entity's massive bell pulses rhythmically, its edges rippling as it swims. Beneath it, hundreds of glowing cyan and deep violet tentacles trail endlessly into the darkness, swaying gently in invisible deep-sea currents generated by procedural noise. Additive blending creates a hauntingly beautiful, ghostly translucency.
