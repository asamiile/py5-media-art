# ambient_mitotic_spindle_formation_3d

## Metadata
- **Date**: 2026-06-20
- **Theme**: Simulating the formation of the mitotic spindle apparatus during cell division (inspired by GO:00002
- **Technique**: A 3D simulation with two glowing centrosome poles at the top and bottom. 150 purple "X"-shaped chromosomes begin scattered and gradually align at the metaphase plate (equator) over the 10-second duration. Green glowing bezier curves representing microtubules extend from both poles to each chromosome, bowing and waving dynamically via 3D noise offsets to simulate physical tension and fluid dynamics inside a dividing cell. Rendered with additive blending.
