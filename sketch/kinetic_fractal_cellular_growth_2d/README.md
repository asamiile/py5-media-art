# kinetic_fractal_cellular_growth_2d

**Date**: 2026-07-05
**Type**: Animation (10-30s @ 60fps)

## Concept
An incredibly detailed, organic visualization of recursive cellular division that morphs into a fractal labyrinth over time.

## Techniques
A variation of the Ulam spiral and cellular automata. Uses highly vectorized Numpy bitwise operations on a grid of integers to simulate rapidly expanding cellular structures. At a certain generation, cells mutate and start emitting secondary fractal patterns, generating a hypnotic, infinite expansion effect.

## Palette
Monochromatic neon. Deep charcoal grey, with glowing hot pink and electric violet cells.
