# Adaptive Nerve Bloom

A neural network visualization exploring the theme of artificial intelligence learning to recognize patterns. The work depicts a network of 180 neurons forming dynamic synaptic connections that strengthen or weaken based on proximity and simulated "learning" signals.

## Visual Concept

The dark canvas blooms with a constellation of cyan, magenta, and gold neural nodes. Connections between neurons pulse with energy as signals propagate through the network, creating an ever-shifting web of computational activity. The animation captures the essence of intelligence emerging and organizing itself in real time.

## Technical Details

- **Format**: Animation (15s @ 60fps, 4K/3840×2160)
- **Algorithm**: Procedural neural network simulation with adaptive synapse dynamics
- **Technique**: 
  - 180 stochastically-positioned neuron nodes
  - Proximity-based connection generation (radius 250px)
  - Activation probability simulation (3% per frame)
  - Distance-weighted synapse strength decay
  - Signal propagation with multi-layer color mapping
  - Particle-like node glow based on activation state

## Color Palette

- **Background**: Deep charcoal (#0f0f0f)
- **Dominant (60%)**: Electric cyan (#00ffdc) with mint highlights
- **Secondary (30%)**: Vibrant magenta (#ff32dc) with deep violet shadows
- **Accent (10%)**: Gold neural highlights (#ffc800)
- **Mood**: Cold/Precise with Neon energy

## Conceptual Theme

This work explores computation and machine learning as visual metaphors for intelligence and adaptation. Rather than simulating a physical phenomenon, it visualizes the abstract beauty of neural networks organizing themselves, learning, and propagating information through complex interconnected structures.

The theme fills a gap in the collection by shifting focus from quantum physics and fluid dynamics to the emerging field of artificial intelligence and computational consciousness.
