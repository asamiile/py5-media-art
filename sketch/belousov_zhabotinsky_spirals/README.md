# belousov_zhabotinsky_spirals

Belousov-Zhabotinsky reaction waves unfold as crimson and amber excitation fronts across a dark chemical medium.

- **Format**: Animation, 20 seconds at 60fps
- **Technique**: Barkley-model excitable medium simulation with vectorized activator/inhibitor updates, periodic Laplacian diffusion, asymmetric spiral seeding, and LANCZOS upscaling from a 512x512 reaction grid.
- **Palette**: Near-black charcoal, deep maroon, crimson, warm amber-ochre, and ivory wavefronts.
