# metabolic_voxels

A 3D generative animation of recursive geometric growth, exploring the concept of "living architecture" and digital metabolism.

## Concept
The work visualizes massive, monolithic structures that grow and breathe in a dark, star-dusted void. Using a recursive growth algorithm, the piece creates complex 3D structures out of glowing voxels. These "synthetic corals" of stone and light subtly pulse in scale, suggesting an underlying metabolic process in a silent, high-tech cosmos.

## Technique
- **3D Recursive Growth (P3D)**: Multiple monolithic structures are generated using a randomized branch-and-extrude algorithm in 3D space.
- **Luminous Edge Rendering**: The facets of the voxels are highlighted with glowing spectral light using additive blending (`py5.ADD`).
- **Dynamic Camera & Breathing**: A rotating camera and a sinusoidal "breathing" scale animation provide a sense of immense scale and organic life.
- **Celestial Atmosphere**: A high-density starfield and deep space lighting address the "beautiful night sky" request.

## Palette
- **Monoliths**: Dark Slate/Graphite (#1e1e32)
- **Edge Highlights**: Electric Cyan, Royal Amethyst, Stellar Gold
- **Void**: Midnight Blue/Black
