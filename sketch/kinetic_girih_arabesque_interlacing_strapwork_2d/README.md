# kinetic_girih_arabesque_interlacing_strapwork_2d

Illuminated medieval Islamic Girih geometric strapwork and continuous over-under ribbon interlacing on antique manuscript vellum, exploring breathing 12-fold and 8-fold rosettes, burnished gold leaf bevels, underpass cast drop shadows, and enameled lapis lazuli and Persian turquoise inlays.

## Concept & Sacred Geometry

Departing completely from physical particle fields and industrial architectural slabs, this work explores the mathematics of Islamic Girih tiling, medieval illuminated manuscript gilding, and topological ribbon interlacing:
1. **Sacred Geometric Symmetry**: Features a monumental 12-fold central Girih rosette interlaced with 6 orbiting 8-fold satellite rosettes and carmine ruby diamond kite inlays, breathing and rotating in harmonic counterpoint.
2. **Topological Over-Under Interlacing**: Classical strapwork ribbon crossings where alternating strands continuously pass over and under one another with physical depth cues, bevel highlights, and drop shadows on underpasses.
3. **Illuminated Manuscript Aesthetics**: Rendered upon warm antique manuscript parchment (`#f6f1e3`) adorned with compass construction circles, 24-ray sacred geometry guidelines, and ornate gilded borders.
4. **Jeweled Zellij Tile Inlays**: Center star rosettes are set with deep lapis lazuli ultramarine (`#162a45`), Persian enameled turquoise (`#0d9488`), and carmine red (`#991b1b`).

## Aesthetics & Color Palette

- **Manuscript Ground**: Antique Gold-Flecked Vellum & Pale Parchment (`#f6f1e3`, `#ede3ce`)
- **Interlacing Strapwork**: Burnished Gold Leaf Highlight (`#fceea8`), Antique Gold Body (`#d4af37`), Shaded Gold Bevel (`#c39b2a`)
- **Enameled Geometric Inlays**: Deep Lapis Lazuli Ultramarine (`#162a45`), Persian Enameled Turquoise (`#0d9488`), Carmine Ruby Red (`#991b1b`)
- **Drafting Contours**: Burnished Walnut Sepia Ink (`#261912`)

## Specifications

- **Format**: Seamless looping animation (720 frames @ 60 FPS, 12 seconds)
- **Resolution**: 3840×2160 (Retina 4K master)
- **Engine**: py5 (Python Processing) + parametric Girih rosette strapwork synthesis + topological over-under crossover rendering + vector drafting
