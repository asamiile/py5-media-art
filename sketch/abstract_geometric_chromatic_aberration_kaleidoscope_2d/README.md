# abstract_geometric_chromatic_aberration_kaleidoscope_2d

An animated 15s sequence of an abstract geometric chromatic aberration kaleidoscope in 2D.

## Concept

A kaleidoscope of sharp interlocking geometric shards slowly rotating, with a simulated lens chromatic aberration effect (RGB separation) at the edges.

## Technique

Additive blending of independent red, green, and blue shard layers. The radial distance controls the spatial separation of the RGB channels, simulating lens distortion.

## Palette

- Background: Pure black (0, 0, 0)
- Shapes: Pure Red, Green, and Blue, blending additively into white where they overlap perfectly.
