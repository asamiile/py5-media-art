# kinetic_stellar_nebula_particle_accretion_2d

A slow simulation of stellar gas and dust accreting to form a star. Millions of low-opacity particles rotating slowly in a massive gravitational well.

## Tech
- **Library**: py5, NumPy
- **Concept**: N-body approximation, orbital mechanics, particle systems, noise turbulence.
- **Palette**: Deep infrared (dark red/orange) transitioning to bright ultraviolet (cyan/blue/white) at the core.
