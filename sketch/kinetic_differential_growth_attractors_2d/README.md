# kinetic_differential_growth_attractors_2d

An animated 2D visualization of differential line growth curves wrapping around moving gravitational attractor wells.

## Details

- **Date**: 2026-08-11
- **Theme**: Luminescent organic cellular structures dividing, expanding, and folding under the gravitational attraction of orbital gravity hubs.
- **Technique**: Vectorized differential line growth (discrete nodes with spring forces, local sampling repulsion, and midpoint splitting division rules) combined with gravitational acceleration mapping to attractor centers. Rendered with double-pass glowing line weights on a trail-faded background.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Background: Pitch Black Void (`#050508`)
  - Dominant: Electric Lime/Mint (`#39ff14`)
  - Secondary: Glowing Deep Indigo (`#4b0082`)
  - Accent: Warm Solar Gold (`#ffd700`)
- **Format**: Animation (60fps)
