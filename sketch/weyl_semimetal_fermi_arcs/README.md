# weyl_semimetal_fermi_arcs

- **Date**: 2026-05-12
- **Theme**: Topological matter, Weyl semimetals, Fermi arcs, chiral Weyl nodes, momentum-space topology.
- **Visual Impression**: Two blindingly bright singularities (Weyl nodes) of opposite topological charge (chirality) float in a dark 3D void. Shimmering, curved filaments of light (Fermi arcs) emerge from one node, sweep across a hidden boundary, and terminate at the other. The arcs pulse and breathe, revealing the hidden connectivity of the material's electronic structure.
- **Technique**: 3D particle simulation. 120,000 tracers. Two centers of attraction/repulsion representing Weyl nodes. Particles follow "Fermi arc" trajectories—semicircular or elliptic paths on a surface in momentum space. Multi-pass additive rendering with P3D projection. Palette: "Electric Cyan / Neon Magenta / Star-White". 20s 4K/60fps MP4 (1080p source).
