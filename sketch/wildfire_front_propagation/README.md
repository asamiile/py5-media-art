# Wildfire Front Propagation

A dynamic visualization of wildfire spreading across dry grassland, showing the chaotic yet mathematically-driven nature of fire expansion and fuel consumption.

## Visual Concept

A spark ignites in darkness and erupts into an expanding wildfire. The flames race across the canvas in organic, chaotic patterns, leaving blackened ash in their wake. Heat gradients shift from incandescent white at the flame core through brilliant orange and deep red to charred boundaries, creating a visceral sense of heat and intensity.

## Technical Details

- **Format**: Animation (18s @ 60fps, 4K/3840×2160)
- **Algorithm**: Cellular automata fire propagation with heat diffusion and fuel depletion
- **Grid Resolution**: 240×135 cells (upscaled to 4K rendering)
- **Technique**: 
  - Probabilistic neighbor-spreading based on heat and fuel
  - Heat diffusion across adjacent cells
  - Temperature-mapped RGB coloring
  - Fuel consumption proportional to heat
  - 3×3 ignition seed for natural starting pattern

## Color Palette

- **Background**: Charred black (#0a0a0a)
- **Dominant (60%)**: Deep burnt orange to vibrant orange (#cc4400 to #ff8800)
- **Secondary (30%)**: Flame red to scarlet (#dd2200 to #ff3333)
- **Accent (10%)**: Incandescent yellow-white (#ffee88)
- **Mood**: Hot, intense, organic chaos

## Conceptual Theme

This work visualizes an authentic natural phenomenon—wildfire propagation—with accurate heat diffusion and fuel depletion dynamics. Rather than stylized glitch effects or abstract physics, it grounds the visualization in real ecological process, creating both mathematical rigor and visceral visual impact.

The piece fills a gap in the collection by exploring large-scale natural disasters with visual intensity while maintaining mechanical authenticity.
