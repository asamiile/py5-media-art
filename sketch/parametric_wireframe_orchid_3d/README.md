# parametric_wireframe_orchid_3d

## Details
- **Date**: 2026-06-03
- **Format**: Animation (15s @ 60fps)
- **Theme**: A delicate, intricate mechanical flower (like an orchid) composed entirely of highly detailed 3D wireframes that slowly bloom and unfold in a mathematically precise way.
- **Technique**: Procedurally drawing parametric surfaces (using sine/cosine/exponential functions for petals) rendered as `py5.LINES`. The flower's petals bend outward continuously by modifying the parameter limits or surface normals based on a sine wave linked to the frame count, simulating a continuous opening and closing bloom.
- **Color palette**:
  - Background: Stark White
  - Dominant (60%): Carbon Black (wireframes)
  - Secondary (30%): Gold (center pistil/stamen accents)
  - Accent (10%): Pale Lilac
  - Mood: botanical / technical

## Description
An animated 3D simulation of a mechanical orchid blooming. Drawn entirely with fine, technical wireframe lines against a stark white background, the petals of the flower curl open and closed in a breathing motion. The outer petals are carbon black, inner petals are pale lilac, and the center is a cluster of striking gold stamens.
