# gluon_flux

A generative media art piece exploring the concept of subatomic particle confinement through the lens of Lattice Gauge Theory.

## Concept
The work visualizes "quarks" held together by "gluon flux tubes." In quantum chromodynamics (QCD), the force between quarks does not decrease with distance; instead, it remains constant or increases, leading to "confinement." This animation simulates that tension, creating a vibrating, high-energy lattice that constantly struggles to maintain its internal equilibrium.

## Technique
- **Lattice Gauge Physics**: Quarks act as nodes in a force-directed graph where spring tension increases with distance ($F \propto r$).
- **Spectral Bloom**: Nodes and edges are rendered using additive blending (`py5.ADD`) to create a shimmering, bioluminescent effect.
- **Tension Visualization**: Flux tubes change color and thickness based on local energy density, flaring into white-gold at peak tension.
- **Atmospheric Depth**: A high-density starfield and deep charcoal background provide a "beautiful night sky" context.

## Palette
- **Background**: Deep Space Charcoal (#050505)
- **Quarks**: Electric Magenta, Cyber Lime, Cobalt Blue
- **Energy**: White-Gold highlights
