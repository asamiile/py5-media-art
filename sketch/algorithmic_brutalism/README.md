# algorithmic_brutalism
 
- **Date**: 2026-05-04
- **Theme**: The stark, massive beauty of industrial logic and architectural recursion; a digital homage to brutalist design.
- **Technique**: Animated recursive quadtree subdivision (P3D) where height and subdivision are driven by a temporal noise field; monolithic slab rendering with deep shadows and safety orange indicator pulses.
- **Logic Lab Reference**: `fractals/quadtree_part_1/quadtree_part_1.py` — used for recursive subdivision logic.

## Description

A heavy, shifting landscape of monolithic concrete slabs that subdivide and merge in a slow, rhythmic "breath." The work explores the aesthetic of "industrial logic," where form is purely a product of algorithmic function. Safety orange lights flicker at the intersections of the largest blocks, suggesting a machine-like intelligence processing deep within the structure.
