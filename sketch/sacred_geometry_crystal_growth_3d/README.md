# sacred_geometry_crystal_growth_3d

## Details
- **Date**: 2026-06-03
- **Format**: Animation (15s @ 60fps)
- **Theme**: A cluster of glowing, translucent geometric crystals (octahedrons and pyramids) growing and shifting rapidly out of a central core.
- **Technique**: Procedurally spawning 3D geometry from a central nucleus. Crystals scale up and rotate outward, blending via additive blending to simulate intense refraction and glowing energy.
- **Color palette**:
  - Background: Very dark purple / black
  - Dominant (60%): Ethereal Amethyst
  - Secondary (30%): Rose Quartz
  - Accent (10%): Pure White energy
  - Mood: mystical / energetic

## Description
An animated 3D simulation of glowing geometric crystals erupting from a central glowing nucleus. Rendered using custom triangulated octahedron geometry, the shards grow, shrink, and translate outwards in a continuous organic cycle, creating a beautiful display of amethyst and rose quartz light using additive blending.
