# Gravitational Warp

A physics-driven visualization of space-time curvature using conformal coordinate warping and gravitational lensing simulations.

![Preview](preview_p1.png)

## Concept
This work explores the visual language of General Relativity. By treating the canvas as a flexible manifold, we simulate the presence of massive singularities that distort the underlying geometric grid. The resulting "Einstein rings" and warped light paths represent the bending of space-time around black holes or heavy galactic clusters.

## Technical Details
- **Coordinate Warping**: Each point on the grid is displaced based on its proximity to multiple simulated mass points.
- **Gravitational Lensing**: The distortion follows an inverse-square law approximation, creating realistic lensing artifacts.
- **Multi-pass Rendering**:
  - Pass 1: Base silver grid representing the undisturbed metric.
  - Pass 2: Sapphire additive glow simulating the blue-shift of light in deep gravitational wells.
- **Einstein Rings**: Concentric circles are subjected to the same warp field, creating the characteristic "lensed" arcs.

## Aesthetics
- **Palette**: Midnight Navy, Sapphire, Lunar Silver, and a touch of Golden Core.
- **Style**: High-tech, mathematical, and ethereal.
