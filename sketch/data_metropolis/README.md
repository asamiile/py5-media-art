# data_metropolis

A generative animation of a sprawling digital city where quantum data packets surge through an intricate geometric grid.

## Concept
The work visualizes the concept of "digital urbanism" — a world where information flow defines the structure of the metropolis. Using recursive subdivision to generate an urban grid, the piece creates a top-down isometric view of a data-driven society. Luminous packets of light navigate the grid, representing the constant exchange of information under a star-dusted night sky.

## Technique
- **Recursive Quadtree Subdivision**: The canvas is subdivided into "city blocks" of varying sizes, creating a complex, non-uniform urban grid.
- **Isometric Projection**: Buildings (blocks) are rendered with pseudo-3D height and shadow faces to create a sense of architectural scale.
- **Data Packet Simulation**: Particles (data packets) are constrained to move along the Manhattan grid of the city, leaving short neon streaks in their wake.
- **High-Density Starfield**: A shimmering starfield background provides a "beautiful night sky" context to the digital landscape.

## Palette
- **Buildings**: Charcoal/Slate (#080808)
- **Data Conduits**: Laser Pink, Cyber Lime, Electric Blue
- **Atmosphere**: Midnight Blue/Black
