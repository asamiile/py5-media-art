# accretion_disk_instability

A majestic visualization of a turbulent accretion disk swirling around a massive compact object.

## Description

Silken filaments of incandescent orange and crimson light spiral into the dark heart of a black hole, creating a complex, shimmering disk that pulses with thermal instability. Violent jets of ultraviolet indigo plasma erupt from the poles, tracing the invisible magnetic conduits that channel energy away from the gravitational abyss against the star-dusted obsidian night.

## Technique

- **Physics**: 3D simulation of 180,000 particles following Keplerian rotation dynamics ($v \propto 1/\sqrt{r}$).
- **Dynamics**: Turbulent noise-driven advection within the disk and high-velocity polar jets.
- **Rendering**: Multi-pass additive point rendering with a high-fidelity HSB palette.
- **Palette**: Incandescent Orange, Crimson Red, and Ultraviolet Indigo.
