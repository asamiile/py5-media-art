# kinetic_physics_magnetic_pendulum_chaos_2d

A visualization of the classic "magnetic pendulum" physics problem. 200 pendulums swing over four magnets, tracing chaotic, fractal-like paths driven by gravity, magnetism, and friction.

## Techniques

A custom physics engine running multiple numerical integration steps per frame. Pendulum trajectories are drawn with motion blur, and their colors are dynamically mapped to the nearest dominant magnet at each moment in time.

## Palette

Deep black background with sharp, vivid primary and secondary colors (Red, Green, Cyan, Purple) blending together through additive synthesis.
