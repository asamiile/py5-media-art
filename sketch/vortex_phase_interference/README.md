# vortex_phase_interference

A mesmerizing visualization of interacting quantum vortex rings in a superfluid medium.

## Description

Two silken filaments of light, representing quantized vortex rings, dance and weave through the cosmic void. As they approach, their phase-velocity fields interfere, creating intricate, shimmering bridges of electric cyan and royal violet energy that pulse against the star-dusted obsidian night.

## Technique

- **Physics**: Simplified 3D vortex dynamics simulating two interacting rings with 150,000 particle tracers.
- **Interference**: Localized velocity field modulation based on ring proximity.
- **Rendering**: Multi-pass additive point rendering with HSB-based spectral mapping.
- **Palette**: Electric Cyan and Royal Violet.
