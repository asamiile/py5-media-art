## Concept

Italian engineer-architect Pier Luigi Nervi (1891–1979) revolutionized 20th-century structural architecture through **ferrocemento** (ferroconcrete reinforced with fine wire mesh) and **isostatic ribbed vaults**. Nervi famously stated that structural form should not be an arbitrary sculptural whim, but the direct, truthful materialization of internal force flows: the ribs of a vaulted cupola follow the exact **isostatic lines** (principal bending stress trajectories $\sigma_1$ and $\sigma_2$).

This artwork translates Nervi's monumental structural kinematics, traveling live-load dynamics, and structural finite-element optics onto a deep blueprint slate substrate (`#0a0e17`):

1. **Monumental Isostatic Lamella Rib Lattice (72 Spiral Ribs)**:
   - 36 left-handed and 36 right-handed logarithmic spiral ribs intersecting in a signature rhombic/diamond mesh spanning a 61.0 m diameter cupola.
   - Rib color mapping reflects continuous localized stress: deep cobalt blue for extreme compressive arch thrust ($\sigma_1$), light structural slate for neutral load balance, and radiant safety amber/crimson for dynamic tensile bending moments.
   - 13 concentric circumferential compression ring ribs stabilizing the vault against lateral buckling.

2. **432 Recessed Precast Concrete Diamond Coffers (*Cassettone*)**:
   - Individual rhombic coffer pans precast with fine wire mesh ferrocemento.
   - Shaded with directional ambient occlusion and dynamic specular sheen as a rotating virtual sunbeam passes overhead.

3. **Central Skylight Oculus (*Lanterna*) & Sweeping Sunbeam**:
   - A monumental 18-spoke compression ring oculus at the vault apex casting a volumetric sunlight cone that rotates $360^\circ$ over the 12.0s cycle.

4. **18 Inclined Perimeter Y-Pylons (*Cavalletti di sostegno*)**:
   - Forked concrete buttresses transmitting the combined gravity thrust and outward lateral reaction forces down to reinforced ground foundation pads.

5. **Four-Corner Structural Engineering Apparatuses**:
   - **Top-Left**: Finite Element Principal Stress Tensor Mohr's Circle tracking dynamic stresses ($\sigma_1(t)$ and $\sigma_2(t)$).
   - **Top-Right**: Catenary Thrust Line of Resistance & Bending Moment Diagram ($M(x, t)$).
   - **Bottom-Left**: Live-Load Modal Vibration Spectrum ($F_{01} = 1.48\text{ Hz}$ radial, $F_{02} = 2.74\text{ Hz}$ torsional).
   - **Bottom-Right**: Ferrocemento Material Specification (8 wire mesh layers, $500\text{ kg/m}^3$ mortar matrix, reinforcement grid).
