# dark_energy_expansion_drift

A majestic visualization of the accelerating expansion of the universe driven by a hidden energy field.

## Description

Silken threads of cosmic lavender and deep emerald light stretch and thin as they drift into the obsidian night. This work simulates the effect of "dark energy" or quintessence, where a repulsive force grows with distance, causing a chaotic-yet-ordered dispersal of energy quanta across the vast intergalactic void.

## Technique

- **Physics**: 3D particle simulation (250,000 points) using vectorized NumPy.
- **Expansion**: Exponential expansion logic $a(t) = e^{Ht}$ where $H$ is the Hubble parameter proxy.
- **Flow**: Multi-harmonic drift field using sine-superposition for fluid-like motion.
- **Rendering**: Multi-pass additive point rendering with life-cycle alpha modulation (fade-in/fade-out).
- **Palette**: Cosmic Lavender, Deep Emerald, Silver, and Platinum.
