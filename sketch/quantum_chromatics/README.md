# quantum_chromatics

A high-energy generative animation exploring the concept of particle collisions and the shimmering decay of subatomic fragments.

## Concept
The work visualizes a rhythmic series of "quantum collisions" at the heart of a star-dusted void. Each event spawns thousands of high-velocity fragments that follow curved paths as if moving through a powerful magnetic field. As the particles lose energy and "decay," their color shifts from a blinding white-gold to electric cyan and royal magenta, creating a vibrant, high-energy tapestry of spectral scattering.

## Technique
- **Collision Event Simulation**: A centralized event triggers the birth of 5,000 fragments with varying initial velocities and charges.
- **Lorentz-Force Path Curvature**: Fragments follow curved trajectories using a simulated magnetic force, with curvature determined by particle charge and velocity.
- **Spectral Decay Mapping**: A temporal color gradient shifts from high-energy white-gold to lower-energy cyan and magenta based on the fragment's age.
- **High-Density Starfield**: A shimmering starfield background addresses the "beautiful night sky" request.

## Palette
- **Core**: White-Gold (#ffffc8)
- **Mid-Energy**: Electric Cyan (#32ffff)
- **Low-Energy**: Royal Magenta (#ff32c8)
- **Void**: Darkest Navy/Black
