# riemann_surface_unfolding

A mesmerizing vision of mathematical beauty; a complex geometric surface unfolds and breathes in the void, its iridescent sheets swirling with silken threads of light that trace the hidden paths of complex functions against the star-dusted obsidian night.

## Concept

This work visualizes a **Riemann Surface**, a multi-sheeted surface in complex analysis that allows multi-valued functions (like square roots or logarithms) to be treated as single-valued. Specifically, it represents the surface for the elliptic curve $w^2 = z^3 - z$, where the complex plane is "unfolded" into multiple connected sheets that meet at branch points.

## Technique

- **Complex Manifold Simulation**: 180,000 particles represent the flow of information across the complex plane.
- **Phase-Gradient Advection**: Particles follow the phase-gradient vector field ($\nabla \phi$) of the complex potential, revealing the topology of the function.
- **Multi-Sheet Topology**: Particles navigate between sheets of the surface based on the analytical continuation of the function.
- **Multi-Pass Additive Rendering**: Captures the iridescent, spectral quality of the sheets with depth-aware HSB mapping.
- **4K Animation**: 20 seconds at 60fps, rendered with FFmpeg.

## Visuals

- **Palette**: Spectral Iridescence (Cyan/Magenta/Gold) that shifts based on the complex phase and surface depth.
- **Motion**: The entire surface "breathes" and rotates, causing the silken currents to swirl and spiral around invisible branch points.
- **Atmosphere**: Mathematical, high-tech, and ethereal, adhering to the "beautiful night sky" aesthetic.
