# atmospheric_veil

A study of noctilucent clouds—the highest clouds in Earth's atmosphere, which glow long after sunset. The work captures the ethereal, rippled texture of ice crystals at the edge of space.

## Concept
Atmospheric light, noctilucent clouds, twilight, ethereal presence.

## Technique
- **Noise Layers**: Three layers of fractional Brownian motion (fBm) noise with varying scales.
- **Domain Warping**: Second-order domain warping to create the characteristic "curled" and "rippled" cloud textures.
- **Spectral Coloring**: A height-dependent color map where the "densest" parts of the clouds transition from deep blue to luminous silver.
- **Solar Edge**: A derivative-based highlight effect simulating the last rays of sunlight hitting the cloud edges.

## Palette
- **Background (Space)**: Deep Indigo / Near Black
- **Veil (Dense)**: Noctilucent Blue
- **Veil (Highlight)**: Pearlescent Silver
- **Solar Edge**: Solar Amber
- **Stars**: Distant White
