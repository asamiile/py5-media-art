# kinetic_math_lorenz_attractor_butterfly_3d

A 3D simulation of the classic Lorenz attractor ("butterfly effect").

## Techniques

50,000 points integrated using the Euler method to trace the Lorenz manifold, leaving glowing trails as they orbit the two butterfly lobes. Rendered with additive blending and a slowly rotating 3D camera.

## Palette

Velocity-based coloring from fiery orange and magenta (fast) down to deep blue/cyan (slow) against a dark background.
