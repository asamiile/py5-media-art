# kinetic_organic_erosion_mesh_2d

An exploration of organic decay through a densely triangulated mesh. Over time, edges in the Delaunay triangulation break according to a shifting Perlin noise field and their vertical position. When nodes lose all their structural connections, they detach and become drifting dust particles that subtly glow as embers in a gentle breeze.

## Tech
- **Library**: py5, NumPy, SciPy (scipy.spatial.Delaunay)
- **Concept**: Organic decay, crumbling structures, cellular destruction.
- **Palette**: Charcoal background with rust, ochre, ash gray, and occasional glowing embers.
