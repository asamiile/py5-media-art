# algorithmic_crystals

A 3D generative animation exploring the concept of digital mineralization, recursive lattices, and geometric resonance.

## Concept
The work visualizes an intricate, shimmering world of synthetic mineralization. Using a recursive lattice growth algorithm, the piece creates glowing digital geodes that grow and pulse in a deep cosmic void. The iridescent surface mapping and internal glow suggest an underlying geometric harmony under a silent, star-dusted night sky.

## Technique
- **3D Recursive Lattice Growth (P3D)**: Generating complex architectural forms using recursive octahedron subdivision and stochastic lattice logic.
- **Iridescent Surface Mapping**: Crystal faces are rendered with an HSB color gradient that shifts based on the local rotation and depth, creating a pearlescent effect.
- **Luminous Internal Glow**: Animate a glowing core inside each crystal that pulses in brightness, suggesting a unified rhythmic beat.
- **Atmospheric Depth & Camera**: A rotating camera and directional lighting create a sense of immense urban scale and dramatic depth.
- **High-Density Starfield**: A shimmering starfield background reinforces the cosmic/astronomical context, addressing the "beautiful night sky" request.

## Palette
- **Crystals**: Molten Gold, Cyber Lime, Royal Amethyst
- **Void**: Midnight Blue/Black
