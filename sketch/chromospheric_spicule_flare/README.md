# chromospheric_spicule_flare

A majestic, high-energy vision of a star's atmosphere; thousands of silken plasma jets erupt from an incandescent amber surface, twisting into complex magnetic loops that snap and flare with ultraviolet light against the star-dusted obsidian void.

## Concept

This work visualizes the **chromosphere** of a star—the dynamic layer above the photosphere where plasma is sculpted by magnetic fields. It focuses on **spicules**, jet-like eruptions of plasma that surge upward at high speeds, and **magnetic reconnection**, a process where magnetic field lines "snap" and release immense energy, triggering solar flares.

## Technique

- **Plasma Spicule Emission**: 120,000 particles are emitted from a hemispherical base, simulating the short-lived vertical jets of the chromosphere.
- **Dynamic Magnetic Field**: A complex, evolving field generated by 8 rotating dipoles advects the plasma into loops and arcs.
- **Magnetic Reconnection Events**: Stochastic flaring events driven by noise triggers intense light and rapid particle acceleration.
- **Multi-Pass Additive Rendering**: Captures the incandescent amber glow of the star and the brilliant ultraviolet and white-gold flashes of the flares.
- **4K Animation**: 15 seconds at 60fps, rendered with FFmpeg.

## Visuals

- **Palette**: Incandescent Amber and Stellar Gold for the main plasma, with sharp Ultraviolet and White-Gold highlights for the flare events.
- **Motion**: Rhythmic vertical eruptions (spicules) are continuously bent and sculpted into swirling magnetic filaments that pulse with energy.
- **Atmosphere**: Intense, high-energy, and majestic, adhering to the "beautiful night sky" aesthetic.
