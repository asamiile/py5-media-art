# quantum_entanglement

A generative animation exploring the concept of non-local connection, particle pairs, and spectral resonance.

## Concept
The work visualizes the intricate, shimmering connections of a quantum system. Using an entangled particle simulation, the piece creates glowing spectral bridges between correlated particle pairs. The phase-locked harmonic pulsing and noise-driven trajectories suggest an underlying quantum coherence under a silent, star-dusted night sky.

## Technique
- **Entangled Particle Simulation (P2D)**: Particles are spawned in correlated pairs with opposite velocities, moving through a complex noise field while maintaining their underlying quantum link.
- **Spectral Connection Bridges**: Glowing, translucent filaments connect each pair, with their visual properties (color, alpha, width) modulated by their spatial separation and local phase.
- **Phase-Locked Harmonic Pulse**: A global rhythm modulates the brightness and color of the connections, suggesting a unified quantum beat.
- **Atmospheric Starfield**: A high-density starfield reinforces the cosmic/astronomical context, addressing the "beautiful night sky" request.

## Palette
- **Connections**: Electric Cyan, Deep Cobalt, Rose-Gold
- **Particles**: Spectral Slate, Pale Silver
- **Void**: Midnight Blue/Black
