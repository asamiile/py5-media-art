# crystalline_bloom

A complex, multi-layered "mineral bloom" that pulses with cold light; thousands of recursive geometric petals are arranged in a perfect Fermat spiral, shifting between shimmering silver and deep amethyst as the structure breathes and rotates.

## Concept
Geometric efflorescence, frozen growth, mineral intelligence. The work captures the feeling of watching a complex mineral structure grow and breathe in a vacuum.

## Technique
- **Phyllotaxis**: Fermat spiral determines the seed positions of the petals.
- **Recursion**: Each petal is a recursive polygon subdivision, adding layers of geometric detail.
- **Animation**: The petals breathe via oscillating scales and rotations, creating a hypnotic, organic rhythm in a 3D space.
