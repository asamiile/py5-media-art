# harmonic_levitation

An intricate visualization of matter responding to invisible resonant frequencies.

## Description

60,000 particles of light navigate a dynamic wave field, assembling into shimmering geometric nodes that morph and pulse as the frequencies shift, creating a delicate, celestial dance against a deep star-dusted void. The work explores the concept of resonant assembly, where order emerges from chaos through the interaction of overlapping oscillations.

## Details

- **Date**: 2026-05-05
- **Theme**: Resonant assembly, acoustic trapping, celestial geometry, beautiful night sky
- **Technique**: 
  - Dynamic multi-source wave interference field (8 oscillators)
  - Gradient-based particle advection towards wave nodes
  - HSB-spectral energy mapping (Amethyst to Cyan to Gold)
  - High-density starfield with twinkle effect
  - Vectorized NumPy computation for high particle counts
- **Output**: 10s animation @ 60fps (3840x2160)
