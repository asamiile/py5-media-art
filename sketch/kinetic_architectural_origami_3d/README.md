# kinetic_architectural_origami_3d

## Details
- **Date**: 2026-06-03
- **Format**: Animation (15s @ 60fps)
- **Theme**: A vast, brutalist architectural structure made of massive geometric panels that unfold and fold like origami.
- **Technique**: A 3D grid of interconnected concrete planes. The rotation of each panel on its hinges is driven by a slow, sweeping 2D noise field. Lighting is harsh and directional to emphasize stark shadows on the brutalist surfaces, using `py5.directional_light()`. The interlocking concrete-like panels fold on alternating axes based on their grid parity.
- **Color palette**:
  - Background: Pale Concrete Grey
  - Dominant (60%): Raw Concrete (mid-grey)
  - Secondary (30%): Deep Shadow (almost black)
  - Accent (10%): Safety Orange (small detail lines/edges)
  - Mood: architectural / brutalist / mechanical

## Description
An animated 3D simulation of a brutalist megastructure continuously folding and unfolding itself. The massive concrete panels pivot on alternating axes, driven by an invisible noise field that propagates wave-like mechanical motion across the surface. Harsh directional lighting casts deep, dramatic shadows over the raw concrete geometry, interrupted occasionally by flashes of safety-orange industrial detailing.
