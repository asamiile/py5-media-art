# cosmic_inflation_pulse

A 3D simulation of the Big Bang's inflationary expansion and the subsequent cooling of the universe.

## Description

A breathtaking visualization of the birth of the cosmos; a blindingly bright point of light erupts into a vast, shimmering web of spectral energy that cools and self-organizes into intricate filaments against the deep, expanding indigo void.

## Technique

- **Physics**: Exponential expansion model $r(t) = r_0 + A(1 - e^{-kt})$ simulating the inflationary phase and transition to standard expansion.
- **Quantum Seeding**: Multi-harmonic interference patterns (using spherical coordinates) to seed density fluctuations that grow into large-scale filaments.
- **Thermodynamics**: Time-dependent color mapping simulating the cooling of the primordial plasma (White -> Yellow -> Cyan -> Amethyst -> Indigo).
- **Rendering**: 3D particle simulation (250,000 points) with distance-based alpha blending and a persistent background starfield for spatial context.
- **Format**: 10-second animation @ 60fps, 4K resolution.
