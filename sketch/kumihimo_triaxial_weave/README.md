# kumihimo_triaxial_weave

## Overview
A 4K (3840×2160) P3D generative video artwork inspired by **triaxial bamboo and silk plaiting (`kumihimo` / `kagome` three-axis weaving)**. Two volumetric 3D canopies of 57 interwoven ribbons (`0°`, `+60°`, `-60°` symmetry axes) float in deep obsidian lacquer space (`#0d1016`), undulating through 1:2:3 poly-rhythmic phase interference over a 16-second (960-frame @ 60fps) seamless loop.

## Visual & Technical Highlights
- **Genre**: Textile / Calligraphy / Procedural Craft
- **Composition Archetype**: 3D Sculptural / Volumetric Space (P3D)
- **Motion Archetype**: Poly-Rhythmic Phase Interference
- **Palette**: Obsidian Lacquer (`#0d1016`), Raw Bone Ivory (`#f4efe4`), Vermilion Lacquer (`#e63826`), Imperial Gold (`#f2b01e`), Cobalt Ultramarine (`#2558d1`).
- **Techniques**: Vectorized 3D ribbon extrusion with true over-under triaxial interlacing and torsion normals in `P3D` (`py5.QUADS` / `py5.vertices`), vertical gold tension filaments (`py5.LINES`), and `PostFX` finishing.
