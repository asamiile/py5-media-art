# dark_matter_halo_cusp

- **Date**: 2026-05-12
- **Theme**: Astrophysics, dark matter, N-body simulation, NFW profile, gravitational collapse.
- **Visual Impression**: A vast, diffuse cloud of "Spectral Indigo" particles—dark matter—shimmers in the deep void. Slowly, driven by invisible gravitational forces, the particles begin to stream towards the center, forming a dense, blindingly bright "cusp". The animation captures the cold, majestic formation of a dark matter halo, the invisible backbone of the cosmic web.
- **Technique**: 3D N-body simulation. 140,000 particles subject to a softened NFW gravitational potential. Multi-pass additive rendering. Palette: "Spectral Indigo / Cosmic Silver / Deep Void". 20s 4K/60fps MP4 (1080p source).
