# pulsar_magnetosphere_flux

Luminous cyan and royal-purple particle filaments twist through a pulsar magnetosphere, opening into a relativistic wind near the light cylinder.

- **Format**: Animation, 20 seconds at 60fps
- **Technique**: 60,000 particle tracers advected through an approximate rotating dipole magnetic field, with toroidal twist near the light cylinder, radial wind for escaping particles, manual 3D-to-2D projection, and additive HSB rendering.
- **Palette**: Deep obsidian background, electric cyan flux lines, blue-white stellar core, and royal-purple outer magnetospheric trails.
