# kinetic_wireframe_hyperspace_tunnel_3d

A high-speed flight through a glowing, twisting hyperspace tunnel. The tunnel is generated as a series of parametric 3D rings that move toward the camera and twist organically, creating an illusion of infinite flight.

## Tech
- **Library**: py5, NumPy
- **Concept**: Hyperspace, 3D wireframe, infinite flight, perspective projection.
- **Palette**: Very dark violet background with bright neon cyan, pink, and white wireframe rings.
