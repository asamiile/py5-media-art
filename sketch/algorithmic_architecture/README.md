# algorithmic_architecture

A 3D generative animation of recursive urbanism, exploring the concept of digital metabolism and synthetic metropolis.

## Concept
The work visualizes a vast, shimmering digital metropolis that grows and pulses in a deep cosmic void. Using a recursive 3D quadtree subdivision algorithm, the piece creates a dense, high-tech cityscape with varying heights and scales. Glowing data highways flow between the buildings, suggesting an underlying digital metabolism under a silent, star-dusted night sky.

## Technique
- **3D Recursive Quadtree Construction (P3D)**: Generating a dense, high-tech cityscape using recursive quadtree subdivision in 3D space, creating complex architectural forms.
- **Luminous Data Highways**: Glowing filaments of light flow between the buildings, representing digital traffic and information exchange.
- **Atmospheric Depth & Camera**: A rotating camera and directional lighting create a sense of immense urban scale and dramatic depth.
- **High-Density Starfield**: A shimmering starfield background reinforces the cosmic/astronomical context, addressing the "beautiful night sky" request.

## Palette
- **Buildings**: Charcoal/Slate (#1e1e28)
- **Data Highways**: Electric Cyan, Royal Amethyst
- **Void**: Midnight Blue/Black
