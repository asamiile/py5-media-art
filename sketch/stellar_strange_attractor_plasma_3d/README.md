# stellar_strange_attractor_plasma_3d

## Details
- **Date**: 2026-06-03
- **Format**: Animation (15s @ 60fps)
- **Theme**: A dense, glowing plasma core representing a magnetic confinement reactor or a star's heart, formed by a shifting strange attractor.
- **Technique**: An implementation of the 3D Aizawa strange attractor. Tens of thousands of points trace the chaotic paths of the attractor in real-time using Euler integration. The primary parameter `c` of the attractor is slowly morphed over time using a sine wave, causing the complex orbital shape to constantly shift and reshape itself. Particles are colored dynamically based on their velocity and position, and drawn with additive blending.
- **Color palette**:
  - Background: Very Dark Crimson / Black
  - Dominant (60%): Fiery Orange
  - Secondary (30%): Solar Yellow
  - Accent (10%): Electric Blue
  - Mood: energetic / stellar

## Description
An animated 3D simulation of a massive, roiling plasma core. Thirty thousand individual particles trace the chaotic orbits of the Aizawa strange attractor. The attractor's parameters slowly morph, causing the stellar structure to expand and contract dynamically. The plasma core glows intensely through additive blending, shifting through deep fiery oranges and bright solar yellows, with electric blue streaks shooting through the highest-velocity streams.
