# superfluid_helicity_resonance

A majestic visualization of quantum fluid dynamics; silken filaments of electric ice and ghostly amethyst light weave and twist in a helical resonance, tracing the hidden paths of superfluid vortex rings as they dance against the star-dusted obsidian void.

## Technical Details

- **Simulation**: 3D particle simulation of 240,000 tracers advected by helical harmonic perturbations (Kelvin waves) along three interacting vortex rings.
- **Rendering**: Manual 3D-to-2D projection with multi-pass additive point rendering to create a soft "ghostly" glow.
- **Palette**: "Electric Ice / Ghostly Amethyst / Deep Cobalt" HSB mapping.
- **Output**: 20-second 4K/60fps animation.
