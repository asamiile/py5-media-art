# generative_kaleidoscopic_mandala_zoom_2d

An animated 15s sequence showing a mesmerizing, jewel-toned mandala rotating and zooming endlessly towards the viewer.

## Theme
An impossibly intricate, infinitely zooming kaleidoscope mandala composed of glowing geometric forms.

## Technique
Symmetrical rotational drawing (12-fold symmetry) combined with a modulo-based infinite zoom scaling trick. Complex nested geometric primitives (polygons, arcs, sweeping lines) are drawn in a single wedge and then rotated and mirrored to form the mandala. The entire structure smoothly rotates and expands.
