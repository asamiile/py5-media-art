- **Technique**: The sketch generates multiple layers of dense geometric primitives: concentric rings and radial lines spanning outwards from the center. ## Concept
A geometric animation that explores optical illusions through the creation of dynamic Moiré patterns. By overlapping and independently rotating/translating dense arrays of concentric rings and radial lines, it produces hypnotic, emergent interference patterns that trick the eye and create a sense of continuous motion and depth.
