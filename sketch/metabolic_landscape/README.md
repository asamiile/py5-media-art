# metabolic_landscape

A 3D generative animation of living geography, exploring the concept of organic planetary terrain and pulsating resonance.

## Concept
The work visualizes a vast, dark planetary surface that appears to breathe and glow. Using a 3D domain-warped terrain algorithm, the piece creates complex, organic ridges and valleys. Shimmering lines of light trace the terrain's contours, pulsing with a rhythmic resonance that suggests an underlying planetary heartbeat under a silent, star-dusted night sky.

## Technique
- **3D Domain-Warped Terrain (P3D)**: A highly detailed 3D mesh generated using second-order domain warping with Simplex noise, creating complex, organic geological forms.
- **Metabolic Contour Pulse**: Glowing contour lines are dynamically extracted and rendered as they traverse the terrain, pulsing in scale and brightness based on a global rhythm.
- **Atmospheric Depth & Camera**: A rotating camera and directional lighting create a sense of immense planetary scale and dramatic depth.
- **High-Density Starfield**: A shimmering starfield background reinforces the cosmic/astronomical context, addressing the "beautiful night sky" request.

## Palette

- **Contours**: Neon Emerald, Electric Amethyst
- **Void**: Midnight Blue/Black
