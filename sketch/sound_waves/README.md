## Concept
Eight layered horizontal sound waves with harmonic overtones, twelve radiating circular wave patterns, and a spectrogram-style background create a complex audio visualization; electric blue, warm magenta, and bright cyan waves interfere against a deep charcoal field with frequency bars at the bottom

## Technical Details
- **Renderer**: P2D
- **Simulation**: procedural waveform generation with frequency-based layering
- **Visuals**: amplitude modulation, harmonic overtones, spectrogram-style visualization
