# relativistic_caustic_drift

A 3D generative media art piece visualizing the complex caustic networks formed by gravitational lensing in a relativistic environment.

## Concept
The work simulates a "light sheet" of 180,000 particles passing through a cluster of invisible gravitational lenses. As the mass centers move, they warp the trajectories of the light rays, creating a shimmering, liquid-like flow of ribbons. Where the light is most concentrated, intense "caustics" appear—needle-sharp lines of blinding brilliance that reveal the underlying curvature of spacetime.

## Technical Details
- **Simulation**: 180,000 particles deflected by 4 dynamic gravitational hubs using a vectorized deflection model $d \theta \propto M/dist^2$.
- **Rendering**: Multi-pass additive rendering with HSB spectral mapping (Cyan -> Silver -> Gold) and influence-weighted intensity.
- **Starfield**: High-density background of 12,000 stars.
- **Resolution**: 3840x2160 (4K) @ 60fps.
- **Duration**: 10 seconds.

## Aesthetic
- **Theme**: Gravitational caustics, relativistic drift, light warping, beautiful night sky.
- **Visuals**: Liquid light ribbons, sharp caustic networks, high-energy focal points against a deep obsidian void.
