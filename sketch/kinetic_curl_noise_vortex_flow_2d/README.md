# Kinetic Curl Noise Vortex Flow 2D

## Concept
Thousands of particles swept up in a turbulent, fluid-like flow generated by Curl Noise. The particles leave glowing trails that twist and fold, mimicking the incompressible chaotic nature of fluid dynamics and smoke.

## Technical Implementation
- Uses `py5.os_noise()` vectorized over numpy arrays to compute analytical gradients of a 3D noise field (using time as the Z-axis).
- The derivatives are used to compute the Curl of the scalar field `(dy, -dx)`, producing a divergence-free (incompressible) vector field.
- 30,000 particles are advected through this field at 60fps.
- Trails are drawn using a fading background technique (`py5.rect` with low alpha). The lines are drawn additively to create a spectrum of glowing ribbons.

## Execution
- `Particles`: 30,000
- `FPS`: 60
- `Duration`: 15 Seconds
- `Resolution`: 3840x2160
